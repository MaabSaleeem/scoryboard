## The findings that shape the collection

Six things were measured in step 1 that the map did not know. All are in
`config/api.md`, "Venues (club locations)", with the calls behind them.

1. **A venue belongs to its creator, and only the creator can change it.**
   `PUT` and `DELETE` from any other account answer
   `403 "Club location can only be modified by its creator"` - and the app's
   Edit form prints that sentence under the Location field. It holds inside a
   tournament: the Owner cannot edit a venue the Admin added, nor the other way
   round. That is what 22.2's `role` flag turns out to mean, and it is the
   article's fourth capture.
2. **Venue lists are per account.** A fresh account searching `Ast` gets `[]`
   although a dozen `KB nn Astro` venues exist on other accounts. Collection
   09's brief said the search "covers every venue on the platform"; it covers
   yours plus the partner rows below. Corrected in `config/api.md`.
3. **Partner venues are global and read-only.** Fourteen rows with
   `source: "Powerleague"` (Battersea, Mill Hill, Milton Keynes, Amsterdam ...)
   come back to every account from a three-letter search, and nobody can edit
   them. 22.1 names them in a sentence and photographs none: the list is the
   partner's and can change.
4. **There are two kinds of venue, and they never meet.** An ordinary venue
   (`isTournament: false`) is what the match form lists. A tournament venue
   (`isTournament: true`) is what the Create Tournament picker lists - but only
   when `saveForFutureTournaments` is also true. With it false the venue is
   **tournament-only**: it answers to `?tournamentId=` and to nothing else, so
   it is invisible in Profile settings, in the match form and in the next
   tournament's picker. Profile settings shows the union of the ordinary and
   saved lists and badges the saved ones **Saved**.
5. **Profile settings is the venue management page.** The map's "adding,
   finding, editing and logos" all live in one block - Leaderboards, Teams and
   Locations -> **Locations** - with Add Location, and Edit / Remove on each
   row's kebab. Remove deletes on the click with **no confirmation**. No other
   screen edits or removes a venue; the match form and the tournament forms
   only add. The route sweep found no `/venues` page.
6. **A logo cannot be removed, and a venue cannot be taken off a match or a
   tournament.** `PUT` without `avatarToken` keeps the logo; `null` and `""` are
   refused. A Scheduled match keeps a deleted venue's id and still prints its
   name (measured before and after the DELETE). A tournament's LOCATION card
   has Edit and no Remove.

Also found, and recorded but not documented: `POST /club-locations` upserts by
name, so adding the same name twice yields one venue; `PATCH` answers 404; the
onboarding `/selectClubLocation` page searches `mil` on open and is reachable
by URL only (collection 01, open question 1).

### 22.2 Tournament-only venues and saving venues for reuse

- Persona: Mo (Owner) for 01-03, Ana (Admin) for 04. Role state: **Owner and
  Admins** can add a venue to a tournament and see the same LOCATION card; only
  the account that added a venue can edit it. Nobody can remove one.
- Preconditions found: findings 1, 4 and 6. The Save for future tournaments
  box is unticked by default and is a `role="checkbox"` button. The picker's `+`
  has no accessible name and must be clicked with `dispatchEvent` (collection
  12, open question 5). The picker lists saved venues only: not the two
  ordinary ones, not the tournament-only ones.
- Dual capture: no. Roles, not plans.
- Steps:
  1. In **Create Tournament**, open **Create or Select Clubs**. Your saved
     venues are listed.
  2. Select **+**, fill in the venue, and tick **Save for future tournaments**
     to keep it. Leave it unticked for a one-off venue.
  3. After the tournament exists, its **Settings -> LOCATION** lists every
     venue on it. Saved marks the reusable ones. **+ Location** adds another.
  4. An Admin can add a venue too, but editing someone else's is refused:
     "Club location can only be modified by its creator".

## Open questions

1. **Is the match form's search meant to find only your own venues?**
   `?query=` returns yours plus Powerleague's; another manager's venue never
   appears, even by exact name. *Assumed in the meantime:* the article says
   "your venues and Powerleague venues" and nothing about other people's.
2. **Is "add the same name twice yields one venue" intended?** `POST` upserts
   by name and ignores the new address. *Assumed:* the article tells the reader
   to edit the existing venue rather than add it again, and does not explain
   why.
3. **Should a tournament's venue be removable?** The card has Edit only; the
   API has `PUT /tournaments/:id {clubLocationIds}` and the seed uses it to
   attach. Passing a **shorter** list to detach one was never tried.
   *Assumed:* the article states that a venue cannot be taken off a
   tournament, which is what every surface in the product does.
4. **What does a match show when its venue is deleted?** Measured once: the
   name still prints. *Assumed:* the article says so and advises editing the
   match to another venue.
