### Why 02.8 uses two accounts rather than one flipped

`config/personas.yaml` captures collections 04 and 18 by flipping one account
between Free and Pro. That works when the pair is one person seeing two versions
of their own screen.

Compare is not that screen. The button only appears while you are looking at
somebody else's profile - it is absent on your own - so the pair is always two
people, and the other person needs statistics or the table is a column of
zeroes. Both halves of 02.8 therefore look at the **same** third player, Otto,
and differ only in who is looking: Pia (Free) and Pru (Pro).

### Where this collection departs from personas.yaml

`personas.yaml` says the player persona is "a member of two teams she does not
own". She is a member of **one**. On the Free plan a team owner may add each
friend to exactly one of their teams - `POST /team-players` answers
`400 ONE_FRIEND_PER_TEAM`, "On free plan, each friend can only be added to one
team" - and Otto is Free on purpose, because a Free owner is what a reader has.
Her Teams panel still holds three: the two every account is born with, plus
KB 02 Rovers.

### The match cannot be undone, and that shapes the seed

Observed on staging, 2026-08-29, the hard way:

- `POST /matches/:id/status` refuses anything once a match is Finished -
  "Cannot update status of a Finished match";
- `PUT` and `DELETE` refuse a match whose date has passed - "Date must be at
  least one hour ahead of the current time";
- a player's statistics **survive the deletion of the team** the match was
  played for. Deleting both teams left the player on three matches.

A match created with a date in the past also **starts itself** a second or two
after it is created, and an event posted before that answers
`400 "Match must be live or paused to add events"` - so that goal is simply
lost, permanently. `scripts/seed-02.mjs` therefore polls the match's own status
before it writes any event, and verifies the resulting statistics at the end.
If the verification fails there is no small repair: `--rebuild` deletes the
three accounts and starts from nothing.

Statistics are also written **asynchronously**. The first read a second after
`Finished` returned all zeroes; the same call a minute later returned the right
numbers. The seed polls, and so does every spec that asserts them.
