## The three things that shape every article here

Read these before the article list; five of the seven articles turn on them.

**1. Creating a match is not one action, and nothing is confirmed.**
**Create Match** fires `POST /matches {"status":"Incomplete"}` on the click and
lands you on `/matches/:id`, a page headed **Match Settings** that is already a
real match row. You then fill in the details and select **Save changes**. Nobody
is asked anything first. A reader who closes the tab at that point has left a
half-built match behind, and that half-built match shows up on their team's
fixture list with a **Finish Setup** button where the details should be. That is
why 09.2 exists, and it is the single most likely support ticket in this
collection.

**2. Seven fields decide whether a match is Incomplete or Scheduled.** Isolated on
staging 2026-08-31 by posting the full body and then dropping one field at a time:

| Field | Missing it leaves the match Incomplete |
|---|---|
| `homeTeam` | yes |
| `awayTeam` | yes |
| `leaderboardId` | yes |
| `clubLocationId` | yes |
| `date` | yes |
| `duration` | yes |
| `teamSize` | yes |
| `tag` (Game type) | **no** - defaults to `friendly` |
| the line-ups | **no** - two empty `players[]` arrays still go Scheduled |

`config/api.md` had the venue half of this ("a match with no venue is Incomplete")
from collection 08. The leaderboard requirement is new, and it is the one a reader
will not guess: **a friendly kickabout still has to be attached to a leaderboard
before the app will call it Scheduled.**

**3. There is no delete, and there is no way back.** `DELETE /matches/:id` answers
`"Match cancelled successfully"` and sets `status: "Cancelled"`. The row survives
and still answers 200 by id; it simply stops appearing in the calendar, in a
team's match lists and in a leaderboard's. And a Scheduled match can never be
pushed back to Incomplete: `PUT {clubLocationId: null}` and `{leaderboardId: null}`
are both refused, `{clubLocationId: ""}` answers "Invalid ObjectId".

Two consequences the specs are built around. **09.5 is retitled** - see the
article list. And **no spec mutates a shared fixture**: every article that
completes, edits or cancels a match creates its own throwaway with
`POST /matches` and cancels it in a `finally`.

### Two seeding traps worth writing down

- **`GET /team-invitations` returns `invitationId`**, not `id` and not
  `teamInvitationId` (which is what the *accept* endpoint's own body calls it).
  Guessing left both second actors sitting on the `/team/join` interstitial for a
  whole run, which every spec that signs them in would have photographed.
- **`isTourCompleted` belongs in the seeded profile.** A new account meets a
  guided tour the first time it opens a match page - a tooltip with **Skip Tour**
  and **Next** floating over the tab strip. The tour is 10.1's subject, not this
  collection's. Skipping it in the app fires `PUT /users/:id {"isTourCompleted":
  true}`, and because that call is a full replace it **clears the account's bio**
  as a side effect (the defect `config/api.md` records for the profile photo). The
  seed sends the flag with the whole profile instead.

### Two capture hazards, both found the hard way

- **A team the reader does not own renders as a placeholder** until the app has
  loaded `/teams` once in that session. Open `/matches/:id` straight from the
  address bar as the plain-Player account and the page reads "Add Away Team",
  "Not set" for the leaderboard and the referee, and "Location not set" for the
  venue - over data that `GET /matches/:id` returns perfectly. Going to `/teams`
  first and coming back renders every one of them. Proved twice each way.
  Collection 08 hit the same store slice, where it showed up as a wrongly applied
  **External** badge. `warm09()` is the fix and every non-owner capture calls it.

  menu button while the match is Incomplete and two once it is Scheduled (Add to

  opened menu says **Cancel Match** - so a change in the app fails the spec
  instead of photographing the wrong menu.

### 09.2 Match statuses, and why a match is stuck on Incomplete

- Persona: manager_pro.
- Preconditions found: a match that is missing at least one of the seven fields.
  The spec builds one rather than using a fixture, because **the change is
  one-way**: a venue and a leaderboard cannot be taken off again.
- The statuses, in prose, with no shot of their own:
  **Incomplete** (details missing), **Scheduled** (complete, waiting),
  **Live**, **Paused**, **Finished**, **Cancelled**.
  `POST /matches/:id/status` moves a match between Scheduled, Live, Paused and
  Finished; it refuses anything at all while the match is Incomplete
  ("Cannot update status of a Incomplete match"), and a Finished match is
  permanent.
- Dual capture: no.
- Steps:
  1. Open the team and look under **UPCOMING MATCHES**. A match that is not
     finished setting up shows dashes and a **Finish Setup** button.
  2. Select **Finish Setup**. You land on **Match Settings** with the details form
     open.
  3. Fill in every field. All seven are needed: both teams, a leaderboard, a
     venue, a date, a duration and a team size.
  4. Select **Save changes**. The form is replaced by the match's details.
  5. Go back to the team. The card now carries the date, the time, the venue and
     **Add to Calendar**.

### 09.4 Match tags, and attaching a match to a leaderboard

- Persona: manager_pro. Role state: **Owner or Administrator** may change either.
  A plain Player gets **Match Preview (View Only)** and no controls at all.
- Preconditions found: **Game type is the tag.** Seven options - Friendly,
  League, Cup, Pre Season, Casual Booking, Bubble Football, League Fixture - and
  **all seven work.** Worth stating, because the API's `tag` enum has fourteen
  values and the mapping is not a plain lower-case: "Pre Season" stores
  `pre season` with a space while "Casual Booking" stores `casualBooking` in camel

  showed it is not.

  2. Choose a type. The badge above the teams changes to match.
  3. Open **Leaderboard** and choose one. Every match needs one.
  4. Save. The leaderboard's name joins the match's details.
  5. Open the leaderboard's **Matches** tab. The fixture is under **Upcoming

### 09.5 Editing or cancelling a match

- Persona: manager_pro. Role state: the gear menu belongs to the **Owner**, an
  **Administrator** and the assigned **referee**. A plain **Player** has no gear
  at all and the page is headed **Match Preview (View Only)**.
- **RETITLED.** There is no delete. The gear offers *Configure appearance*, *Edit*
  and *Cancel Match*; the API's own `DELETE /matches/:id` answers "Match cancelled
  successfully" and sets `status: "Cancelled"`, leaving the row in place.
- Preconditions found:
  - The gear appears on an Incomplete match too, with the same three items - so a
    half-built match **can** be cancelled from the app. **Add to Calendar** and
    **Show Tour** are the two header controls that only appear once the match is
    Scheduled.
  - The Edit dialog **prefills** from a Scheduled match: leaderboard, both teams,
    date, duration, team size, start time, venue, pitch, game type and note. On an
    **Incomplete** match it opens blank, with placeholder crests reading YT and OT,
    because there is nothing to prefill. **Referee never prefills**, even when the
    match has one - see 09.6.
  - The Cancel Match window says "This action cannot be undone. This will
    permanently cancel the match." At the API a Cancelled match can in fact be put
    back with `POST /matches/:id/status {"status":"Scheduled"}` - but **no control

    stands. Recorded here so nobody re-discovers it and thinks the article is

- Dual capture: no. Role capture: yes, one shot.
- The Cancel Match window is photographed on the seeded fixture **without being

- Steps:
  1. Open the match and select the gear.
  2. Choose **Edit**. **Update your match** opens with the match's details already

  3. Change what you need and select **Update Match**.
  4. To call the match off, select the gear and choose **Cancel Match**.

### 09.6 Assigning a referee, and adding a banner or note

- Persona: manager_pro. Role state: Owner and Administrator can do all three. The
  referee themselves gets the gear and can edit the note.
- **Referee - narrowed, and this is the important part.** The **Referee** field in
  the Edit dialog is a search box over
  `GET /team-players/search?searchType=referee&tournamentSelectionOnly=true`. That
  last parameter narrows the results to referees **you** have saved, and the only
  call that saves one is `POST /tournaments/:id/referee` with
  `saveForFutureTournaments: true` - a tournament call. A football manager with no
  tournament therefore reads **"No results found"** whatever they type, for ever.
  Verified: `isReferee` is false on a plain account and settable through nothing a
  user can reach (`PUT /users/:id {isReferee}` is ignored, `defaultProfile:
  "Referee"` changes a different field, and `PATCH
  /players/:id/referee-settings` answers 403 to anybody but that player).
  So the article shows the field, says why the list is empty, and shows a match
  that already has a referee. It does **not** show a reader choosing one, because
  no reader can. The seed puts Rae on the fixture with
  `PUT /matches/:id {refereePlayerId}`, which accepts any playerId. See open
  question 1.
- **Banner** = the gear menu's **Configure appearance** window: an *Upload a cover
  image* tile and a *Select a theme* list. The theme list has exactly one entry,
  **Default Theme**, which is worth saying rather than implying there is a choice.
- **Note** has two homes. It is a field in the Edit dialog (250 characters, with a
  counter), and it is editable in place on the **FEED** panel: **Add Note** when

### Corrections to this brief, made mid-run

Five. Each was a step-1 claim the app disagreed with.

1. **09.4 and 09.5, shot 05 - "Match Preview (View Only)".** The brief promised
   the reader would see that label. The page's `h1` really does read *Match
   Preview (View Only)* - and it is `display: none` at every desktop width, so a

   there, the label is not on screen) and the shot descriptions say what a plain
   Player actually gets: the match read-only, with no gear, no **START MATCH** and
   no form. The away team is badged **External**.
2. **09.6, shot 01 - the empty referee list.** Opened cold the list reads *No

   taken on a throwaway with no referee, which is every match a reader is about to
   set up.
3. **09.4, shot 02 - the Game type badge.** The badge does not change when you

4. **09.7, shot 06 - the Day view.** Switching to Day lands on today, and both

### One capture that breaks a screenshot rule on purpose

`docs/style-guide.md` lists "a spinner, skeleton or half-rendered chart" under
**What must not appear**. 09.7's shot 02 is nothing but skeletons - because the
skeletons *are* the subject. The Share window calls that link the one to put on
social media, and a signed-out visitor gets a Sign In button and grey placeholders
that never resolve. Held twenty seconds, no console error, checked from a fresh
browser context. The rule exists to stop half-loaded pages being passed off as
finished ones; here the half-loaded page is the finding.

### Cancelled matches left behind

The suite creates six throwaway matches across a full run and cancels every one.
A cancelled match cannot be deleted, so they accumulate on
`kb-manager-pro-09@yopmail.com` for ever - invisible in the calendar, in the
team's fixture lists and in the leaderboard's. Harmless, and worth knowing before
somebody counts rows in the database and worries.
