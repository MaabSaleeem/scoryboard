### What the `role` flag means here

Collections 12 and 13 settled this for the whole tournament board and it has not
changed:

- **Owner and Admin see the same board** at `/tournaments/:id/<tab>` - nine tabs,
  plus the settings page by URL.
- **Everybody else is redirected** to `/tournaments/:id/info`, which is the
  six-tab public page inside the signed-in shell. Verified again this session as
  `kb-15-outsider@`: asking for `/tournaments/<cup>/results` lands on
  `/tournaments/<cup>/info`.

Endpoints observed and appended to `config/api.md` this session, all marked
`(observed in app, 2026-09-01)`:

- `PATCH /tournaments/:id/chat/settings {"announcementOnly":bool}` - the Postman
  export has only the GET, which answers
  `{conversationId, announcementOnly, chatEnabled}`.
- `POST /tournaments/:id/follow` - no body; a second call answers
  `409 "Already following this tournament"`. `GET` answers
  `{followerCount, isFollowing}`.
- `PUT /tournaments/:id {presentation}` - the whole object, sent by all three
  halves of the Presentation tab.
- `POST /tournaments/:id/presentation/gallery` (multipart, field `picture`) and
  `POST /tournaments/:id/presentation/attachments` (multipart, field
  `attachment`, any MIME type).
- `GET /tournaments/:id/presentation/attachments/:filename?download=1` - what a
  public attachment link points at.
- `POST /matches/:id/status {"status":"Scheduled"}` answers
  `400 "Cannot update status of a Finished match"`. There is no reset endpoint.

the public Matches tab came back reading "No upcoming matches found", because every
fixture was finished and the knockout ones are Incomplete. Scoring one group of two
gives the public page six finished fixtures and six upcoming ones, which is what a
tournament in progress looks like. It also leaves `canEndPhase` false, so no phase
banner appears on Results - those controls are 13.11's article.

**Group B is re-timed after it is regenerated.** Regenerating restarts a group at
the tournament's own kick-off time, which would put Group B on top of Group A. The
seed puts it back to 11:00-11:50 with the same bulk call the Schedule tab sends.

**Fixed dates.** 24 Oct, 17 Oct, 31 Oct, 21 Nov, 7 Nov and 14 Nov 2026. A
tournament match starts itself when its kick-off arrives and a match that arrives
Finished at 0-0 can never be scored (collection 10), so the seed throws rather
than building an unscoreable board if the dates are ever edited into the past.

### 15.1 Your public tournament page

- Persona: organiser, Owner, plus a browser context that has never signed in.
- **RETITLED.** The map says "Publishing your tournament and your public page".
  There is nothing to publish. `GET /tournaments` answers `isPublic: true` on
  every row, no screen carries a control for it, and the public page exists from
  the moment the tournament does - the same shape as 12.1's finding that there is
  no Draft status.
- Preconditions found:
  - The public page **really is public**. A signed-out visitor gets the whole
    thing, with `Sign In` where the sidebar would be. That is unlike the
    leaderboard and match "public links", which send a stranger to `/signin`
    (collections 08 and 09).
  - Six tabs: INFO, PARTICIPANTS, STANDINGS, MATCHES, LEADERBOARD, CHAT. The
    labels are upper case by CSS and title case in the markup.
  - **Standings opens on the last phase**, so a Group & Knockout tournament lands
    on the bracket and the reader has to pick Group Phase to see the table.
  - The Info tab is generated from the setup: date, start time, total teams,
    number of groups, total matches, number of locations, each location, format.
  - A tournament nobody has configured still has a page, and it reads in zeros.
- Dual capture: no.
- Steps:
  1. Open your tournament from **Tournament** in the sidebar. The nine tabs are
     your board; nobody else sees them.
  2. Use **Share tournament** in the header to get the public link. 15.2 covers
     what is in that dialog.
  3. Open the link. Anybody can, signed in or not.
  4. **Info** is the summary, built from your setup.
  5. **Standings** opens on your last phase. Choose **Group Phase** for the group
     table.
  6. **Matches** splits into Past Matches and Upcoming Matches.
  7. **Participants** lists the teams, and their players and referees.
  8. **Leaderboard** ranks the teams and names the leader.
  9. Before you have set anything up, the page still exists and reads in zeros.

## Dual Free/Pro capture

**15.7 only, and the finding is that the plan makes no difference.**

`config/api.md`'s limits table says "Reading incoming chat in full, and
attachments" is blocked on Free, with a `CHAT_PRO_REQUIRED` modal titled "Unlock
full chat with Pro". The tournament group chat is not where that happens.

Tested with kb-15-free@ (Free, never flipped) and kb-15-admin@ (Pro), on the same
long message in the same room:

- both see the message truncated with a **Read more** control;
- Read more expands it in place, for both;
- no modal, no upgrade prompt, no `CHAT_PRO_REQUIRED`;
- both get a composer and both get the attachment control.

So the two captures are 03 (Pro) and 04 (Free), and the article says the two are
the same rather than inventing a limit. `kb-15-free@` is Free **at rest** and is
never flipped, per `config/personas.yaml`.

Where the plan might still bite is an attachment rather than text - the limits
table names attachments too - and nothing in this collection uploads one to a
chat. Flagged under Open questions 3.

## Open questions

1. **The Prizes tab's Winner picker does not work, and 15.6's prizes half is
   dropped because of it.** The tab is headed **Winner** - "Choose the team that
   should be saved as the tournament winner" - with a `CURRENT WINNER` panel and
   **Set Winner**. The dialog lists the teams in standings order. Clicking one
   leaves the trigger reading "Select a team", leaves the hidden native select
   empty, and leaves **Save Winner** disabled, so no winner can ever be saved.

   Established rather than assumed: the option receives
   `pointermove, pointerdown, mousedown, pointerup, mouseup, click`, every one
   `isTrusted: true`, and the listbox does not even close; no item is
   `data-disabled`; it fails the same way on a tournament with real standings and
   on one with none; and the **control test passes** - the Group component's own
   "Select group" Radix Select, on the same board, in the slideshow editor, takes
   the identical click and moves from "Select group" to "Group A".

   `GET /tournaments/:id` carries `winnerTeam: null`. No request is made when Save
   Winner is pressed, so **the call that saves a winner has never been observed**
   and is not written down.

   Assumed in the meantime: 15.6 covers sponsors only and says nothing about
   prizes or winners. **This is the one thing in the collection a human has to
   decide**: whether to hold 15.6 until the picker is fixed, or publish the
   sponsors article as it stands.

2. **What mints a `/tournaments/token/:token` access link?** The route exists, it
   is on the auth guard's public list, and it renders "This tournament access
   link is invalid or expired." for anything that is not a token. Nothing in the
   organiser board produces one - not Share Tournament, not Add Player, not Add
   Referee, not the settings page. Assumed in the meantime: 15.2 documents the
   link and the QR code, and shows the expired screen as the only thing a reader
   will actually meet.

3. **Does the Free chat limit bite on an attachment?** Open question 2's twin.
   The limits table names attachments alongside reading in full, and no capture in
   this collection puts an image in a chat. Assumed: the article says nothing about
   attachments and plans. Collection 18 owns chat and should settle it.

4. **`presentation.website.infoBody` is written but never read back.** The PUT
   sends it and the app renders it on the public page, but `GET /tournaments/:id`
   does not return the field. The seed and 15.3 both work around it by trusting
   the write. Harmless today; worth knowing if a later collection tries to read
   it.

5. **The public Matches tab labels a link "Match Settings" for everybody,
   including a signed-out visitor** - and it is not settings. Followed with no
   session: it opens `/match/:id/preview`, the read-only match preview collection
   09 documented. So it is a mislabelled link, not a permission leak. It is
   visible in 15.1/06, and 15.1's prose says plainly what it opens rather than
   leaving a reviewer to wonder.
