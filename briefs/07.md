## The finding that shapes this collection

**Teams is the collection of one-way actions, and two of the map's titles
describe features that do not exist.**

Accepting an invitation, claiming a team, removing a member, blocking them and
deleting a team cannot be undone from the reader's side, and several cannot be
undone from the API's side either. So **no spec in this collection performs any
of them.** Each is photographed as the control, and the confirmation dialog where
there is one, and stopped there. Every "after" state comes from a second fixture
`scripts/seed-07.mjs` has already put in that state. That is the pattern
collections 12 and 13 used for ending a phase and deleting a tournament.

Three things exploration settled that change what gets written:

1. **There is no ownership transfer.** The role list the app offers is exactly
   `[{Player},{Administrator}]`; there is no Owner option anywhere and no
   transfer mutation in the bundle. A team changes hands only by being created
   unowned and then claimed. **07.9 is retitled.**
2. **There is no Fan role in the web app.** The API takes `role: "Fan"` and hides
   those rows behind `?includeFans=true`, but the app's `TeamRole` enum has no
   Fan, nothing in the UI can create one, and a Fan row renders with **no role
   badge at all**. **07.7 is retitled** and no fixture holds a Fan.
3. **Removing a member is not confirmed and leaves nothing visible.** Both
   *Remove from team* and *Remove & Block* fire on the click - there is no "are
   you sure". The removed row survives in the API flagged `isDeleted` but no
   screen renders it, so there is no blocked-members list to photograph. What the
   block actually does is stop that person letting themselves back in.

All three are written up in `config/api.md` under `(observed in app,
2026-08-29)`, with the isolations that produced them.

### Why 07.7 is retitled

The map's title promises four roles. The product has three the reader can see and
two they can set.

`POST /team-players` accepts `role: "Fan"` and stores it; `GET /teams/:id/players`
hides Fan rows unless `?includeFans=true`. Both verified on one team. But the web
app's own enum is `Owner | Administrator | Player | Admin | Team | Referee |
Padel` - no Fan. The role dropdown on every dialog offers Player and
Administrator only, and a seeded Fan row renders on the Edit Team page with no
badge where the others carry one.

So a Fan section would document a state no reader can reach, illustrated by a row
that looks like a rendering fault. The article covers Owner, Administrator and
Player, and says plainly that Owner is set by creating or claiming the team and
cannot be given away.

**`config/articles.yaml` keeps the mapped title**, the way 13.8, 14.5 and 01.3
were handled. The Fan question goes in the end-of-run report.

### Why 07.9 is retitled

Searched the bundle three ways: no `transfer`, no "make owner", no "new owner",
and the mutation list is create, update, delete, claim, add/remove/update player,
remove-and-block, bulk invite, invite, join. The role options are a literal
two-element array. `userRoles.filter(v => v !== TeamRole.Owner)` exists for an
Administrator acting on somebody, but `Owner` is not in `userRoles` to begin with,
so it filters nothing.

The article covers claiming, which is the only way a team changes hands, and its
last screenshot is the evidence for the negative: the Role list with two options
in it.

no statistics at all - isolated on staging with two identical finished matches,
one with and one without. Without it, 07.10's entire subject is a row of zeroes.

the dead row first and does nothing when it is there. Without that, KB 07 Athletic
would grow one more removed member on every run.

# What step 2 changed - 2026-08-29

Written during the run, as `docs/workflow.md` requires. Nothing above was
rewritten; this is the record of where execution differed from the plan.

## One thing the brief got wrong about a screenshot

**07.5/06's note about the share code is moot.** The brief argued that the code
should be shown rather than masked, because the article is about the link you
send somebody. It turns out the field is too narrow for it: the input clips at
`https://staging-sb.app.scoryboard.c`, and no part of the code is ever on screen.
Nothing was masked and nothing needed to be. The reasoning stands if the field is
ever widened.

## Seven capture defects, all fixed in the specs

- **The team crests came out as initials.** Every row on Manage Teams paints the
  team's coloured initials first and swaps in the crest when the image loads, and
  `imagesPainted()` cannot see an `<img>` that is not in the DOM yet. Five shots
  across 07.1, 07.2, 07.4, 07.6 and 07.9 showed "KU" where KB 07 United's badge
  belongs. `teamListReady()` now waits for that one crest.
- **The same race on the Join a team card**, fixed the same way in 07.6.
- **07.4/06 clipped the sidebar, not the card.** `teamCard()` matched the
  heading page-wide, and the sidebar's navigation carries a "Leaderboards" link -
  so the LEADERBOARDS shot came out a 510px-wide strip of the sidebar.
  `teamCard()` is scoped to `main` now.
- **07.1/08 cut its own subject off.** A new team lands at the foot of the list,
  which is below the fold, so the row's size and role badges were clipped by the
  viewport edge. `centre()` first.
- **07.9/01 had a black bar across the search results.** The identity mask paints
  at the element's own coordinates, and the open search dropdown covers them - so
  the block landed on top of the list it was meant to sit beside. Hidden rather
  than masked, which is the call collection 02 made about the notification badge
  on the same screen. New helper: `hideSidebarIdentity()`.
- **07.1/04 and 07.3/03 left the persona's name legible.** Both are viewport
  captures of a dialog, so the sidebar is still in frame. Masks added.

## Three things learned about writing specs for this app

- **A spec cannot switch accounts inside one test.** `signInAs()` mints a fresh
  session, but the browser context still holds the first account's and the app
  stays signed in as whoever got there first. 07.2, 07.6, 07.7 and 07.8 are two
  `test()` blocks each for that reason; each test gets its own context.
- **`signInAs()` assumes a sidebar.** It proves the session by waiting for the
  `/tournaments` link, which `/team/join` does not have. New helper
  `signInBare()` signs in on `/teams` first and then navigates.
- **`shot()` gained a `fullPage` option**, for 07.4/02 - the one capture in this
  collection that documents a whole page rather than a control.

## The Radix selects here do NOT need the dispatched click

Collections 12, 13 and 14 all found controls that only respond to
`dispatchEvent('click')`. The three selects in this collection - team size,
member role, the friend list - open on a plain click. What they do need is not
pressing Escape afterwards: Escape closes the whole modal, not just the list.

## Nothing else differed

Eleven articles, 63 screenshots, exactly the shot lists above. Nothing was added,
nothing was dropped, and no article was published that the brief did not cover.
