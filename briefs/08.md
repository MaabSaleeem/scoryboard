# Collection 08 - Leaderboards & leagues

Persona default: manager_pro   Feature flag: none
Articles in map: 5   Articles in brief: 5   Screenshots planned: 30

A leaderboard is Scoryboard's league. It holds teams, it collects the matches
tagged into it, and everything it displays - the table, the player grid, the
fixture list - is derived from matches played inside it. Nothing here is gated
behind a feature flag; two things are gated behind Pro.

### The two things in this fixture that cannot be repaired

Both cost a `--rebuild`, which deletes all four accounts and replays everything.

1. **A played match.** `POST /matches/:id/status` refuses anything once the match
   is Finished; `PUT` and `DELETE` refuse a match whose date has passed. The seed
   verifies the table at the end and exits non-zero if it is wrong.
2. **A comment.** `DELETE /comments/:commentId` exists but answers `401
   "Unauthorized to delete this comment"` even to the account that wrote it. The
   only way to clear a thread is to delete the leaderboard it hangs off - which
   orphans the matches, so it means a full rebuild.

The first run of the seed hit the second one: `GET /comments` returns **top-level
comments only**, a reply is invisible to that listing, and the reply was posted
twice. `ensureComments()` now reads `GET /comments/:id/replies` as well. The
duplicate was cleared by `--rebuild`. **Do not weaken that check.**

### 08.5 Sharing, commenting on and deleting a leaderboard

- Persona: Mo (Owner).
- Preconditions found:
  - The **share control is an unlabelled round icon button on the card's banner**,
    beside the kebab. `border-white` is the only thing that distinguishes it in
    the DOM. There is no share control on the board page itself, and the card's
    kebab menu holds only **Edit** and **Remove**.
  - The **Share Leaderboard** window offers a read-only `Link`
    (`<origin>/leaderboards/<id>`), a copy button and a QR code, under "People
    with this link can view your board but can't change it. This is the link you
    should share on social media, on your website or elsewhere."
  - **A signed-out visitor who opens that link is redirected to `/signin`.** The
    link is shareable, not public. The article must say so - the window does not.
  - The QR code's own `<title>` reads "Scan the QR code to view this tournament"
    on a leaderboard. A product slip; not quoted.
  - **Commenting is members-only.** `POST /comments` answers `403 "Only
    leaderboard members can comment on or like leaderboard content"` to anybody
    who is neither Owner, Administrator, nor a player on one of its teams -
    although the composer is still drawn for them. Liking is gated the same way.
  - The composer is a `textarea` placeholdered `Write your comment...`, beside
    **Add Media** and a **Comment** submit. Replies are nested behind "View n more
    replies".
  - **Deleting is confirmed**, from either entry point - the settings screen's
    DELETE LEADERBOARD section, or **Remove** on the card's kebab menu. Both open
    the same window: "This action cannot be undone. This will permanently delete
    the leaderboard and remove all associated data." / Cancel / Delete Leaderboard.
- Dual capture: no.
- Role: only the Owner and an Administrator can delete. Only a league member can
  comment or like. Anybody signed in can read the comments.
- Steps:
  1. On **Leaderboards**, select the share control on the card.
  2. Copy the **Link**, or let somebody scan the QR code. They will need a
     Scoryboard account to open it.
  3. On the board, scroll to **Comments** and type in the box.
  4. Select **Comment**.
  5. To delete it, open the settings screen and select **Delete Leaderboard**,
     then confirm.

## Open questions

1. **Can an Administrator actually delete the leaderboard?** They are given the
   whole settings screen including the DELETE LEADERBOARD section and its button.
   Not tested: confirming it would destroy four unrepeatable matches. Assumed for
   the articles that they can, because the UI offers it without qualification, and
   08.2 and 08.5 both say the control belongs to "the Owner or an Administrator".
   If the API refuses an Administrator, 08.5's role line is wrong.
2. **Why does `adminPlayers[].isRegistered` read `false` for an account that
   exists?** Ada has a Scoryboard account, is linked by playerId, and reads the
   board with `isAdmin: true`, yet the row says she is unregistered. Assumed to be
   a bug in that field; no article repeats it.
3. **Is the default banner image picked from the id?** A leaderboard with no
   banner gets one of `/images/leaderboard/1.png` to `6.png`. Two different
   leaderboards got `1.png` and `2.png`. If it is derived from the id, the banner
   changes on every `--rebuild`. docs/style-guide.md permits that kind of
   difference between runs; no spec asserts the image.
4. **Is `Leaderboard style` ever anything but "Football leaderboard"?** It is
   disabled on both screens that show it, and the field is required by
   `createLeaderboardSchema`. Presumably it would read differently for a Padel
   leaderboard, if one can be made. Assumed there is one value and no way to
   change it; 08.1 and 08.2 both say so.
5. **What does the UI show a non-member who submits a comment?** The API answers
   403 with a clear message. Not driven through the browser, because the refusal
   would raise an error toast and the style guide forbids photographing one that
   is not the article's subject. 08.5 states the rule from the API's message.
