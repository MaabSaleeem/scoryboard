## The finding that shapes the whole collection

**A referee cannot make themselves one.** There is no referee sign-up, no referee
route and no referee screen. `/referees` is an API path, not an app route - it
answers 404 from the server, and none of the 36 entries in the app's own `Routes`
enum is a referee page.

Exactly one call in the whole product sets `isReferee`, and it belongs to a
tournament organiser: `POST /tournaments/:id/referee`. Everything a referee then
sees is the **ordinary player profile in a different role** - a blue
**Referee / Football** switch on the profile header flips the bio, the
statistics, the leaderboard count and the match lists to their referee
equivalents.

So the three articles are: how you become one and what appears (21.1), where your
assignments show up (21.2), and what you may do on match day (21.3).

Two consequences worth having in mind while reading the rest:

- **Nobody tells you.** Being added as a referee sends no notification and no
  email. Verified both ways - see 21.2.
- **The match page does not read `isReferee` at all.** It reads
  `refereePlayerId` on that match. Being a Scoryboard referee and being *this
  match's* referee are two different things, and only the second one gives you
  controls.

### Why 21.2 is retitled

The map's title promises an invitation to accept. Four measurements say there is
none:

1. `POST /tournaments/:id/referee` sends **no notification.** The target account
   held 0 notifications before the call and 0 after it.
2. **No email either.** One yopmail read of `kb-referee-21@` returned three
   messages, all "Welcome to Scoryboard, set your password" from the three
   `POST /admins/users` calls that made and remade the account. Nothing from the
   referee add.
3. **None of the eighteen notification types is referee-related.** The full enum
   is in `config/api.md`, printed by the API in its own validation error.
4. **There is no accept endpoint and no pending state.** The only accept in the
   bundle is `/team-invitations/accept`, which is for team players.
   `refereePlayers[]` on the tournament carries `isRegistered`,
   `saveForFutureTournaments` and `canStartEndMatches` and nothing that could
   hold "invited but not yet accepted". `GET /team-invitations` as the referee
   answers an empty list.

A referee is **added**, not invited, and they are live the moment the organiser
saves. The article says that in its first line, because a reader waiting for an
invitation will wait for ever. The retitle follows the precedent set by 11.1,
17.9 and three of collection 20's four titles.

### Why 21.3's content is not what the reference said

`config/api.md` carried a role matrix saying the assigned referee gets **no**
score steppers and **no** Yellow / Red / Player of Match. Both were wrong, and
21.3 is the article that would have published them. Re-measured today, side by
side with the Owner on the same Live match:

- the score stepper row is **identical** for referee and Owner - one
  blue-bordered pill, four 32px buttons, both minus buttons `disabled` at zero
  and both plus buttons enabled;
- clicking the referee's plus fires `POST /matches/:id/events
  {"type":"GoalAwarded"}` and answers **200**. The server score moved 0-0 to 1-0;
- **Yellow Card, Red Card and Player of Match sit on the referee's FEED** as soon
  as the match is Live.

`isReferee` is irrelevant: a plain account that had never been made a referee,
named on a match with `PUT /matches/:id {refereePlayerId}`, got the same page -
five tabs, no heading, the steppers, the card buttons, Show Tour and the timer.
That is almost certainly how the old row came about, since collections 09 and 10
both measured from an account that was only named on a match.

`config/api.md` is corrected, with today's date and the evidence. **No published
article repeated the wrong claim** - I checked every collection-10 article body
for it, and 10.4's "You are not the Owner, an Administrator or the referee" is
about who gets controls at all, which is right. So nothing live needs a fix.

What the referee genuinely cannot do, isolated on a throwaway match with the
referee's own token:

| Call | Answer |
|---|---|
| `POST /matches/:id/status` - Live, Paused, Live, Finished | **200** each |
| `POST /matches/:id/events` - goal, card, Player of the Match | **200** |
| `PUT /matches/:id` - `pitchNumber`, `note`, `date`, `status:"Cancelled"` | **403** `"Only match managers can update match settings"` |
| `DELETE /matches/:id` | **403** `"Only match managers can cancel match"` |

**A referee runs the match and cannot change the match.** That is 21.3's sentence.

### Two traps in the seeding itself

**`createMode` is required on `POST /tournaments/:id/referee`, and a call without
it half-succeeds.** Sent without it the call answers `400 "Referee not found"` -
having already written `isReferee: true` and `defaultProfile: "Referee"` to the
target user. That left this collection's first Rae marked as a referee, on no
tournament and in nobody's saved list, and every later add for her address then
failed the same way. The only fix was `DELETE /admins/user-delete` and start
again, which is what the seed's `--rebuild` does. Worth a ticket.

**The Add referee dialog's Multiple referees tab is broken on the server.** The
client posts `{createMode:"multiple", refereeList:"a\nb", ...}` and the API answers
`400 SCHEMA_VALIDATION_ERROR {"field":"name","message":"Required"}`. Not this
collection's article - it is the organiser's side, so collection 13 or 15 - but
worth a ticket, and it corrects `config/api.md`'s claim that the tab "fires one
POST per line".

### Why all three matches are rebuilt every run

A match cannot be un-started and cannot be un-finished, and this collection needs
one Scheduled, one Live and one Finished match on screen at once. So the seed
disposes of last run's and makes three new ones.

Disposal is `PUT /matches/:id {"status":"Cancelled"}`, not `DELETE`:
`DELETE /matches/:id` refuses anything whose date has passed, and a cancelled row
stops appearing in every listing. **That matters beyond tidiness** - 21.2
photographs Rae's Past list, and without the sweep every run would add a row to
it. The teardown sweeps twice, the referee listing and the owner's own listing,
because an exploration can leave a match on the fixture teams that somebody else
referees.

### The clock, and the two dates that are fixed

`MATCH_DATE` = `2026-12-05T19:00:00Z` and `LIVE_DATE` = `2026-12-06T19:00:00Z`,
both in `lib/fixtures-21.mjs`. **The seed refuses to run once either has passed**,
and refuses if `LIVE_DATE` is not after `MATCH_DATE`.

Three reasons they are fixed and ordered:

1. A match whose date has arrived **starts itself** (`config/api.md`, "The match
   lifecycle"), and 21.3's first half needs a match that has not kicked off.
2. Both sit in `scheduleType=Upcoming`, and the profile's REFEREED MATCHES panel
   renders **only the nearest one**. If the Live match were the earlier of the
   two, 21.2's first capture would photograph it instead of the Scheduled one.
3. `/schedule` opens on the current month. The two calendar captures freeze the
   clock at `2026-12-01T09:00:00Z` so the grid is December 2026 every run, and
   both fixtures are in it.

**The Live match's kick-off is deliberately NOT fixed.** The server stamps
`startedAt` when `POST /status` runs. 21.3's Live captures freeze the page clock
at `startedAt + 12 minutes`, read off the seeded match, which pins the timer at
**48:00** without any date being hardcoded - collection 10's pattern. The END
MATCH capture freezes at `startedAt + 60 minutes + 5s`.

**One date cannot be fixed and is masked instead**: the `played` match's own.
Events are accepted only while a match is Live, and a match with a fixed past date
is Live for about a second, so it has to be dated 90 seconds back and polled. Its
date and time cell is masked on 21.2's Past capture, which the style guide allows
for "absolute dates and times that are not the point of the screenshot" - the
point of that capture is the score and the fact that the match is in your Past
list.

### 21.3 Refereeing a match - what you can and cannot do

- Persona: `referee` (Rae). Role state: named as `refereePlayerId` on all three
  fixture matches.
- Preconditions found - the whole of "Why 21.3's content is not what the reference
  said" above, plus:
  - The referee's match page has **five tabs** - MATCH DETAILS, FEED, FACTS,
    LINEUP, KEYS. **No PAYMENT tab at all.** The Owner has six plus two PAYMENT
    sub-tabs.
  - **No heading.** The Owner and Administrators get "Match Settings" and everyone
    else gets "Match Preview (View Only)"; a referee gets neither.
  - **No gear menu.** Measured on a Scheduled match: the Owner has three
    `button[aria-haspopup="menu"]`, the referee has two - Add to Calendar and the
    footer's language picker. There is no Configure appearance / Edit /
    Cancel Match. This corrects `config/api.md` **and** `briefs/09.md`, which said
    the referee "gets the gear and can edit the note".
  - **Add Note is a silent no-op for a referee, and a reader will hit it.** The
    FEED gives a referee **Add Note**, the dialog opens ("Add Note", a
    250-character box, Save and Close), Save sends `PUT /matches/:id {note}`, the
    API answers 403 - and the dialog closes with **no message on screen** and the
    note still reads "No Note added." Worth a ticket. It is the whole of 21.3's
    "If it does not work" section.
  - Card buttons appear **only while the match is Live**. On a Scheduled match the
    FEED carries Comment alone.
  - **END MATCH appears only at full time**, when the timer reaches 00:00. Before
    that the timer pill is what is there, and the pill is also the pause control.
  - At full time the MATCH DETAILS card reads **"Match auto-ends in 1d 23h ..."** -
    the 24-hour grace period from the other side, which collection 10 documented.
  - The guided tour opens over the match page on an account that has never
    dismissed it, and its overlay swallows every click. The seed sets
    `isTourCompleted: true` on all three accounts. It cost this collection's first
    stepper probe a 20-second timeout.
- Dual capture: no.
- Steps:
  1. Open the match from your profile or from Schedule. There is no heading and no
     gear - you cannot edit the match.
  2. There are five tabs. There is no PAYMENT tab.
  3. Select **START MATCH** at kick-off.
  4. The button becomes the timer. Select it to pause, and again to resume.
  5. Add goals with **+** and **−** under the score.
  6. Award **Yellow Card**, **Red Card** and **Player of Match** on the FEED.
  7. At full time select **END MATCH**.

- **No spec in this collection presses START MATCH, END MATCH, a stepper, a card
  button or Save on the Add Note dialog.** Every one of them is a one-way mutation
  and `docs/style-guide.md` forbids a spec that only works on its first run:
  "Photograph the dialog, do not submit it. Take the after state from a second
  fixture that is already in that state." The three fixture matches ARE those
  states. The exploration pressed a stepper once, on purpose, to settle whether it
  works; the seed rebuilt the match afterwards.

## Open questions

1. **Is the referee's Add Note button meant to be there?** The control is
   rendered, the dialog works, and the save is refused with no message. Either the
   button should be hidden from a referee or the API should accept the note.
   *Assumed in the meantime:* the button is a mistake and the 403 is the intended
   rule, so 21.3 tells the reader the note is the Owner's to set. Worth a ticket.
2. ~~**Is `See All` on the REFEREED MATCHES panel unfinished?**~~ **ANSWERED
   during step 2: no, it works.** It opens the Refereed Matches modal. The
   step-1 measurement was wrong; see "Amended during step 2".
3. **Should TOTAL FOULS be there at all?** The tile is fed a field the endpoint
   does not return. *Assumed:* an API gap rather than a UI mistake, since fouls
   are a referee's natural statistic. 21.1 says the tile stays at zero.
4. **Why did the first `POST /tournaments/:id/referee` write `isReferee` before
   validating?** Measured, reproducible in its effect, and the cause is
   server-side. *Assumed:* the handler updates the user and then looks up a
   referee record that does not exist yet. Recorded so the next session does not
   burn an account on it.
5. **Does `canStartEndMatches` change anything outside a tournament?** The
   dialog's helper text says "Allows manual tournament match control from
   Results", and the bundle only consults it on a tournament match
   (`tournament.canRefereeStartEndMatches`). Rae has it set to true, so this
   collection never sees it off. *Assumed:* it has no effect on an ordinary match,
   and 21.3 says nothing about it. **This is the thing to look hard at** - if the
   flag does gate START MATCH on a tournament fixture, a referee with it off will
   read 21.3 and find no button. Nadia Whistle is seeded with it off, so the
   contrast is available to a future session without new seeding.

## Unreachable

- **A referee on a TOURNAMENT match.** KB 21 Cup has no teams and no saved format,
  so it has no fixtures, and giving it any would make this collection a tournament
  collection. Everything 21.3 documents is an ordinary match, which is the case a
  reader hits. Open question 5 is the part that stays unmeasured because of it.
- **The Multiple referees tab working.** The server refuses it. Recorded as a
  defect, not documented as a step.
- **Any screen that lists more than one of a referee's upcoming matches, other
  than the calendar.** The panel draws one and See All is dead.
