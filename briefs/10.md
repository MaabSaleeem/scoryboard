## The five things that shape every article here

Read these before the article list. All five were isolated on staging on
2026-09-01, one match at a time, and all five are now in `config/api.md` under
[The match lifecycle](../config/api.md).

**1. A match runs itself.** A match starts when its date arrives - `autoStarted:
true`, a server-set `startedAt`, about two seconds after `POST /matches` answers,
with no browser open. And **a match that would already have ended is created
finished**: the rule, isolated across three durations, is that
`date + duration` more than 24 hours in the past means the row arrives `Finished`,
`autoFinished: true`, `0-0`, and nothing can ever be written to it. That is
article 10.9, and it cost this collection's first seed six of seven events.

**2. A Finished match cannot be reopened or rewritten - but it can be cancelled.**
`POST /status` answers "Cannot update status of a Finished match" and a `PUT` of any
configuration field answers "Cannot update match of a Finished match". There is no
undo anywhere. So the `played` fixture is built once and photographed for ever, and
**every article that starts, scores, cards or ends a match builds its own
throwaway.**

`PUT /matches/:id {"status":"Cancelled"}` is the exception, and this brief had it
wrong for half the run: it answers **200 on a Finished match**, because `status` is
not one of the configuration fields that message guards. That is why every throwaway
is cleared in a `finally` and nothing accumulates.

**3. A Live match cannot be edited.** `PUT /matches/:id` with any configuration
field answers `403`, "Cannot update match configuration fields when match is Live".
Date, venue, duration, team size, line-up: all of it locks at kick-off. The gear
menu still offers **Edit**, and its save is refused. 10.2 and 10.3 both say so.

**4. The whistle is one button that changes four times.** Beside the tab strip:
**START MATCH** while the match is Scheduled; the **timer pill** while it is Live,
counting *down* from the duration, with a pause icon; the same pill with a play
icon while Paused; **END MATCH** once the timer reaches 00:00; and nothing at all
once it is Finished. The pill *is* the pause control - there is no separate one -
and **END MATCH does not exist until full time**. Two exploration runs went looking
for it in the gear menu and in the page body before the clock was pushed forward.

**5. There are no penalties and there is no final-score field.** Both belong to a
tournament knockout: `isPenalty` and `hasScoreEntry` are read in the bundle behind
`tournamentMatchId`, beside the copy "Enter a deciding score for ... to proceed with
the next round of the tournament". On an ordinary match the score is the two
`+` / `-` steppers, and a 0-0 ends as a **DRAW** with no prompt of any kind -
confirmed by ending one. **This is why 10.6 is retitled.** See the article list.

### Three seeding traps worth writing down

- **A line-up position that repeats has to carry its index.** `CenterBack-1` and
  `CenterBack-2`, not `CenterBack` twice. Two players on the plain value are
  accepted by the API and stored intact, and the pitch then draws **an empty `+`
  slot for each centre-back and neither player** - the board keys its slots by
  position and the two collide. The first build of this fixture showed three of
  five starters. Cost a `--rebuild`.
- **Commentary is `type: "Comment"`.** `POST /events {minute, description}` with no
  type answers `400 SCHEMA_VALIDATION_ERROR`, `field: "type"`. That closes the TODO
  `config/api.md` had been carrying for 10.6 and 10.8.
- **The `played` fixture cannot have a fixed date**, and the seed says so at
  length. Events land only while a match is Live; a match is Live only between its
  date and its date plus its duration; so a fixed date months back is Live for
  about a second and finishes itself with the events half written. The seed dates
  it five minutes before it runs and the specs read the date back.

**Why there are two leaderboards.** A match cannot leave `Incomplete` without a
`leaderboardId`, so even a throwaway needs one - and a match that finishes inside a
leaderboard writes into its table and into both teams' records. If the throwaways
went into KB 10 Sunday League, the *Statistics so far* half of the FACTS panel on the
Scheduled fixture would read differently on every run, and 10.1 photographs that
panel. They go into KB 10 Midweek instead, and **no article photographs a list of a
team's matches.**

The throwaway leaderboard is **named like a real second league on purpose.** It was
"KB 10 Scratch" until the first full capture run, and then it turned up in the detail
strip of eight published screenshots - 10.3, 10.5, 10.6, 10.7, 10.8, 10.9 and 10.10
all photograph a throwaway, and every one of them shows its leaderboard's name. A
reader should see a second league a manager might plausibly run, not this
collection's scaffolding. Renamed with one `PUT /leaderboards/:id {name}` and
everything re-captured.

**Nothing accumulates.** This paragraph said the opposite for half the run, and the
opposite was wrong. `PUT /matches/:id {"status":"Cancelled"}` answers **200 on a
Finished match** - what a Finished match refuses is being reopened or rewritten, and
`status` is not one of the configuration fields that 403 guards. So every throwaway
this collection creates is cleared, whatever state it ended in, and a full run leaves
the two fixtures it started with. Verified: `node scripts/seed-10.mjs` reports two
matches visible and zero writes after a complete suite.

**Three frozen clocks, all derived, none written down.** There is no `FROZEN_NOW`
constant in this collection and there cannot be one, because the `played`
fixture's date is whatever moment the seed ran. `fixtures10()` derives
`frozenNow` as `played.date + 3h`, which is what every capture of the two shared
fixtures uses: the feed then reads a stable three hours behind the reader and the
Scheduled fixture's countdown reads the same on every run. A capture of a Live
throwaway freezes to `startedAt + 12 minutes` instead, so the timer reads
**48:00** - it counts down from 60 - and a capture of full time freezes to
`startedAt + 61 minutes`, which is what puts **END MATCH** on screen.

## Open questions

1. **How far back the 24-hour rule really reaches, and whose clock it uses.** The
   boundary was measured at between 24.75 and 25.13 hours before "now" for a
   60-minute match, and the same once the duration is subtracted at 90 and 120
   minutes - so "ended more than 24 hours ago" fits every measurement. Assumed
   that, and 10.9 says "more than a day ago" rather than quoting a figure. What is
   not known is whether the server measures in UTC, in the venue's timezone, or
   against the account's, and whether the worker that does it runs on a schedule
   that could delay the effect. A reader in a different timezone recording a match
   near the boundary might get a different answer.
2. **Whether the referee may end a match as well as start one.** Confirmed that an
   assigned referee gets START MATCH and the timer pill on a Scheduled and a Live
   match. **END MATCH was not exercised as the referee** - it only appears at full
   time, and putting a refereed throwaway there needs the referee's own frozen
   clock. Assumed the referee may, because it is the same button in the same place;
   10.3 says "the referee can start, pause and end" and this is the one line in the
   collection resting on inference rather than observation. Flagged in the report.
3. **`/goal/revoke`.** A path literal in the app bundle. Nothing observed on the
   wire uses it - revoking posts `type: "GoalRevoked"` to `/matches/:id/events`.
   Assumed dead or partner-integration only. Not documented anywhere.
4. **The livestream placeholder on the FEED panel** - "Keep your followers up to
   date by starting Livestream / Share video, texts, and score updates". No control
   was found that starts one, and there is no endpoint for it. Assumed unbuilt.
   10.1 shows it in shot 03 and says nothing about it; no article claims a
   livestream can be started.
5. **The Rate control on a finished match.** A star rating with "0 reviews"
   appears beside the heading once a match is Finished, backed by
   `POST /ratings {entityType: "match"}`. No article in this collection covers it
   and the map has no article for it. It is visible in 10.3 shot 05 and 10.6 shot
   07. Possibly collection 19's (comments and ratings). Raised, not documented.

## Changed during the run

Nobody vetted this brief before step 2, so everything the run learned that
contradicts what is written above is collected here as well as corrected in place.
Nothing was changed quietly.

### The retitle, and the two things that are not in the product

- **10.6 retitled** to "Yellow and red cards, and how the final score is set". An
  ordinary match has no penalty control and no typed final-score field. Both are
  tournament-knockout features - `isPenalty` and `hasScoreEntry` are read behind
  `tournamentMatchId` - and a 0-0 ends as a plain **DRAW** with nothing asked.
  Decided in step 1 and unchanged since.

### Corrections to the fixture

- **A line-up position that repeats needs its index.** `CenterBack-1` /
  `CenterBack-2`. The first build used plain `CenterBack` twice, the API accepted
  it, and the pitch drew an empty slot for each centre-back and neither player -
  three of five starters visible. Fixed and `--rebuild`.
- **The throwaway leaderboard was renamed** from `KB 10 Scratch` to
  `KB 10 Midweek`, after the first full capture run showed it in the detail strip
  of eight screenshots. Everything re-captured.
- **`PUT {"status":"Cancelled"}` works on a Finished match**, so nothing
  accumulates. The brief and the seed both said the opposite; both corrected, and
  `config/api.md` now records what a Finished match actually refuses.

### Corrections to the specs, and the traps behind them

Each of these cost a failed run, and each is written into the spec that hit it.

- **A substitute slot's label is not clickable.** Only the `+` beside it carries the
  handler. The first `subSlot()` matched every `div.flex.flex-col` containing the
  label - which includes the whole pitch column - and `.first()` then returned one
  of the eleven other clickable divs inside it.
- **The ONLINE pill is CSS upper case.** Its real text is `🟢 Online 1`, so
  `getByText(/ONLINE \d+/)` matched nothing, the mask painted nothing, and the pill
  went into a capture. Fourth instance of this trap in the project after GROUP A,
  DELETE ACCOUNT and the MATCH DETAILS caption.
- **Playwright's `hasText` string filter is a case-insensitive substring.**
  `matchDialog10(page, 'Add Media')` also matched the **Add comment** window beneath
  it, because that window contains the words "Add media". `gate10()` now anchors on
  the FREE Upgrade button instead.
- **A day cell in the date picker is identified by `data-day="dd/MM/yyyy"`**, not by
  an accessible name of "12" - its name is the whole date spelled out.
- **The card windows disable Select Player until a team is chosen.** *Select team* is
  marked required and the player field is not; clicking straight at the player field
  waits out its whole timeout. This is article content, not just a spec fix: 10.6's
  steps put the team first.
- **`imagesPainted()` in `lib/kb.ts` was dropping its probe `Image` objects**, so
  the browser could collect them before they loaded and neither `onload` nor
  `onerror` ever ran - the wait then timed out on two background images that both
  answered 200 when fetched by hand. Intermittent by nature, which is how it
  survived nine collections. The probes are now retained and the wait polls on a
  timer rather than on `requestAnimationFrame`. **This fix is shared by every
  collection**, so it is worth knowing about outside this one.

### Two things about the timer that changed what the shots show

- **The timer runs from `startedAt`, not from the kick-off time in the details.**
  A frozen clock set to `date + 12 min` read 51:03 rather than 48:00, because the
  server had started the match three minutes after its nominal kick-off.
  `afterKickOff()` takes `startedAt`.
- **A paused match's timer cannot be pinned by a test clock, so 10.3 shot 03 and
  10.4 shot 03 mask the digits.** While the match is Live the pill is drawn from
  the browser clock; the moment it is paused it is drawn from the `pausedAt` the
  server stamps, and the server ignores a `pausedAt` sent in the body. One run read
  48:00 live and 59:40 paused, one second apart. **A real user never sees that
  jump** - their clock and the server's agree - so this is a capture artefact, not
  a product fault, and the fix is to mask a number that is not the subject rather
  than to write a warning nobody needs. The play icon is what the shot keeps.
- **Pausing and ending had to be split into separate tests.** Pausing under a frozen
  clock makes the app record a pause of about eleven minutes, and full time then
  moves out by that much - time paused really is added back, which is the article's
  own claim, but one test cannot demonstrate two clock-sensitive behaviours at once.

### Three product findings the brief did not have

1. **Reloading a paused match resumes it.** The page decides on load that a match
   inside its own window should be running and posts `{status: "Live"}`. Proved
   three times on staging: Paused before the reload, Live after it. A manager who
   pauses at half-time and refreshes has restarted the clock. **A real defect,
   worth a ticket**, and 10.3 both warns about it and asserts it.
2. **The app names the 24-hour rule itself.** Once a match passes full time the
   countdown in the MATCH DETAILS card changes from "Match starts in" to
   **"Match auto-ends in"** and runs for 24 hours. So the boundary 10.9 measured
   from the outside is the same grace period the screen advertises from the inside:
   a manager has a day to enter the score, and a match that finished more than a day
   ago has already missed it. Recorded in `config/api.md`.
3. **The FACTS panel's Insights sentences are not leaderboard-scoped.** *Statistics
   so far* is - it reads the match's own leaderboard, so the throwaways in KB 10
   Midweek leave it alone - but the five Insights sentences per team count that
   team's last five games across everything. So 10.1 shot 04 will read "0
   consecutive goals" on one run and "1" on the next, and a re-capture will differ.
   Harmless: no article quotes those numbers, and the panel itself is collection
   11's subject. Named here so a future session is not surprised by it.
