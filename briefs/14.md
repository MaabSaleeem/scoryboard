## What the map got wrong

Each of these was checked in the rendered app on 2026-08-28, not inferred.

1. **"deleting fixtures" (14.5) does not exist.** A tournament fixture cannot be
   deleted or cancelled anywhere. The Schedule tab's cards carry a status chip, a
   **View** button and four editable cells - and no menu. **View** goes to
   `/match/:id/preview`, which is titled **"Match Preview (View Only)"** and has
   no menu either. The bundle does hold `DELETE /matches/:id` behind a **Cancel
   Match** action, but that action belongs to the *general* match card used
   elsewhere in the product; it is not rendered for a tournament fixture. Checked
   twice: no ellipsis control on either screen.

   The map's own note for 14.5 already reduced the article to "SHARED. Same
   controls on both - SELECT MATCH TO UPDATE, BULK MATCH UPDATE", which is
   consistent with deletion having been speculative. **Retitled to "Rescheduling
   fixtures, and rolling them onto the next day".** The article says plainly that
   fixtures cannot be deleted, and that the only way to remove one is to delete
   its group - which is 13.3 - or to change the format.

2. **"the gap between rounds" (14.4) is not in the bulk dialog.** It is
   **"Gap between rounds (minutes)"** in the **Padel Configuration** dialog on the
   Format tab, the dialog 13.10 documents. The bulk dialog's own
   *Time between matches* field is disabled on padel for the reason below. 14.4
   therefore covers the bulk dialog for rounds and courts, and steps across to
   Format for the gap.

3. **Football is not locked into a sequence and padel is not locked into
   rounds.** The map reads as though the two sports schedule differently by
   nature. They do not: **the same dialog serves both**, and the only difference
   in it is one word - football says **Pitch number**, padel says **Court
   number**. What differs is the *generated* default, and either can be turned
   into the other. A football group given "Same start time per round" puts every
   fixture on one kick-off; a padel group with the box unticked spreads its rounds
   into a flat sequence. Both were done on the wire.

4. **14.8's dialog offers Excel as well as PDF**, and exporting needs Tournament
   Pro. On the Basic plan the button opens an upgrade dialog reading
   "Tournament Pro is required to export fixtures." The article keeps its mapped
   title, names Excel in a sentence, and states the Pro requirement as a
   precondition.

## The gap field is disabled exactly when it is needed

This is a product defect, it is reproducible, and four articles depend on it.

**What the control promises.** The Bulk Match Updates dialog has a checkbox,
**Same start time per round**, with the help text *"Matches in one round share a
start time. The next round starts after the round gap."*

**What the UI does.** The box defaults to **ticked** (`sameStartTimePerRound: !0`
in the bundle's `defaultValues`). While it is ticked, **Duration** and **Time
between matches** are both disabled - `disabled: isSubmitting ||
watch("sameStartTimePerRound")`. So through the UI there is no way to set the
round gap the help text refers to.

**What the server does.** `PUT /tournament-groups/:id` with
`sameStartTimePerRound: true` **and** a `timeBetweenMatches` behaves exactly as
promised - each round shares a start time and the next round begins one gap
later. Sent `{sameStartTimePerRound: true, duration: "15 min",
timeBetweenMatches: "25 min"}` to a padel group and got rounds at 09:00, 09:25,
09:50, 10:15. The server is right; the form will not let you send it.

**The consequence.** Ticked, with no gap to send, every fixture in the group
lands on **one kick-off time**. On a football group that is worse still, because
a football group has no rounds at all - so the whole group counts as one round.
Six fixtures at 10:00, with one team needed in three of them at once. That is
14.6's clash, and it is what the dialog's own default does to a football group.

Recommended for a ticket, not an article. No article tells the reader to work
around it; 14.3 tells them to untick the box, which is the real fix, and 14.6
explains the state they land in if they do not.

### Why the two clash fixtures are seeded rather than made by a spec

Collection 13's determinism note: *"A spec that mutates its fixture must put it
back itself."* The corollary is that a fixture three articles photograph should
not be broken by a fourth. 14.6 and 14.7 need a broken schedule to point at, so
they get their own tournaments and touch nothing.

### How a mutated fixture is restored

`lib/fixtures-14.mjs` holds the target states and the restore calls, and both the
seed and the specs import it - so a spec cannot drift from what the seed expects.

- **KB 14 League** is put back with the same `PUT /tournament-groups/:id` the
  bulk dialog sends. 14.3 and 14.5 each do it at the end of their own run.
- **KB 14 Padel Open** has to be **regenerated**, not re-timed. A bulk update
  writes ONE court number across every match it touches, so once a group has been
  forced onto court 1 nothing but a regeneration restores "Round 1 - Court 1" on
  court 1 and "Round 1 - Court 2" on court 2. Re-sending an identical padel
  configuration does **not** regenerate - verified - so the gap is nudged by one
  minute and put straight back.

## How the times are worked out

Read off the wire, and the arithmetic every article depends on.

- **Sequential** (`sameStartTimePerRound: false`): each kick-off is the previous
  one plus **duration + gap**. Duration 15 and gap 20 gives 10:00, 10:35, 11:10.
- **Per round** (`sameStartTimePerRound: true`, gap sent): every match in a round
  shares a kick-off, and the next round starts one **gap** later - the duration is
  not added. This is reachable over the API only; see the defect above.
- **Per round, no gap** - what the UI always sends: **every fixture in the group
  gets the same kick-off time**.
- **The generated padel schedule** steps by **duration + `padelRoundGapMinutes`**,
  which is why the seeded rounds are 20 minutes apart on a 10-minute gap.
- **`endTime`** is a per-day ceiling, not an end: when the next kick-off would
  pass it, scheduling moves to the next day and starts again at `startTime`.
  Six fixtures, 35 minutes apart, from 10:00 with a ceiling of 11:00, fill 26, 27
  and 28 September two at a time.

Court and pitch numbers are assigned by the **generator**, one per match. The
bulk dialog's Court/Pitch number field writes a **single** value across every
match it touches - it cannot distribute them.

### 14.6 Why two football fixtures clash, and fixtures showing only one team

## Determinism

Built on collection 13's three rules, all of which applied again:

1. **A spec that mutates its fixture puts it back itself.** 14.3, 14.4 and 14.5
   all re-time a schedule another article photographs. Each restores at the end,
   using the values in `lib/fixtures-14.mjs` that the seed also reads - so a spec
   cannot drift from what the seed expects.
2. **Clipped captures need a known scroll position.** `shot()` already scrolls to
   the top first.
3. **Gate on the fixture list, not on its container.** The Schedule tab paints
   its phase tabs, its EXPORT FIXTURES button and each group's header - including
   SELECT MATCH TO UPDATE - before the fixtures arrive. `scheduleReady()` waits
   for a fixture's own status chip.

Two things this collection added:

- **`scheduleReady()` accepts `Incomplete` as well as `Scheduled`**, because a
  knockout fixture and a one-team fixture never read Scheduled. The `.first()`
  goes on the *outside* of the `or`: KB 14 Clash Cup shows both chips at once, and
  an or of two single locators then resolves to two elements and fails strictly.
- **Upper case on this tab is CSS, not content.** GROUP A, BULK MATCH UPDATE,
  ROUND 1 and EXPORT FIXTURES are all `text-transform: uppercase`, so their
  accessible names are `Group A`, `Bulk Match Update`, `Round 1` and
  `Export Fixtures`. Matching the rendered casing finds nothing. The articles use
  the rendered casing, the specs use the DOM's.

### What the bands actually are

The Schedule tab renders a round-robin football group's fixtures in bands. The
branch that does it is keyed on `gameType === "Football"` **and**
`format === "RoundRobin"` - nothing else in the product renders a band, so
`KB 14 Cup` has none.

| Fact | Measured |
|---|---|
| Where `UNSCHEDULED` sits | **last, under the weeks.** The null week is sorted to the end |
| When it exists | only while a fixture in that group has no date |
| What a drop on it sends | `PUT /matches/:id {"date": null}` |
| What the card then reads | `Incomplete`, and all four cells empty |
| What a drop on a week sends | `PUT /matches/:id {"date": <the earliest date already in that week>}` |
| If that week has no dated fixture | the tournament's `startDate` at midnight, plus 7 days per week of offset |
| How it is driven | native HTML5 drag and drop, payload `application/x-scoryboard-football-match` |

### `14.3` said the band sits above the weeks. It does not.

That sentence went live on 2026-09-13 in the A13 pass and is wrong: the app sorts
the null week **last**. Corrected here to "That band is the last one, under the
weeks", with a pointer to 14.5 for how a fixture gets there. **Prose only** -
14.3's shot 05 photographs a group with no undated fixture, so no band appears in
it and no image is affected.

### Two things 8sept-updates.md B6 gets wrong

Both were checked before anything was written, and the article follows the
measurement, not the note.

1. **"The toast reads `Match moved to Unscheduled`."** No toast renders. The
   bundle asks for one on every branch of the drop handler - `Match unscheduled`,
   `Match rescheduled`, `Match cannot be moved` - and none of them reaches the
   DOM. Polled every 400ms for five seconds after a drop that demonstrably
   worked, with `quiet()`'s toast-hiding stylesheet removed so it could not be
   this repo's doing: nothing appeared but Next's route announcer. The article
   therefore says *"Nothing else confirms the move: the card changing bands is
   the confirmation."*
2. **"The reverse drag lands the fixture on the tournament start date at midnight
   and renders `0:00`."** Only when the week you drop into holds no dated
   fixture. The normal case is worse in a different way: the fixture takes the
   **earliest kick-off already in that week**, so it lands on top of an existing
   fixture and makes the clash 14.6 is about. The article carries both, and
   quotes no clock face - midnight UTC renders `1:00` in British Summer Time, so
   `0:00` is not what a reader here sees.

### The precondition, and why it is honest

The band is the drop target and it only exists once something is in it, so the
spec clears **one** fixture's date over the API before it drags a **second** one
by mouse. That is not a shortcut around the reader's own action: a freshly
generated round-robin arrives with every fixture undated and the whole group
sitting in that band - 8sept-updates.md A4 - so "at least one fixture has no
date" is the state this flow starts from in real life. The article says so in as
many words before its first step.

### The restore is per match, not per group

`restoreGroupSchedule()` is **not** used by this test, and that is deliberate.
The group bulk update re-times a group in the server's own order, and a null date
perturbs that order: the first run of this work put the same six pairings back
against different kick-off times. That would have changed 14.3's shot 05 and this
article's own 01 to 04 on their next capture, for no reason.

So the test records all six dates up front and `PUT`s each one back in a
`finally`. Verified afterwards: the six published shots of this collection that
photograph `KB 14 League` and `KB 14 Cup` re-captured **pixel-identical** to what
is live - 0.00% of subpixels differ on all six - so the fixture is exactly where
it started.
