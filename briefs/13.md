## What the map got wrong

Each of these was checked in the rendered app on 2026-08-28, not inferred.

1. **"Fill automatically and Clear have no football equivalent"** - true, but they
   are **group** controls, not bracket controls. Both sit in the padel Group
   Phase card, above Group A, and they act on the group draw:
   `PUT /tournament-groups/:id {"applyToPhase":true,"clearAssignments":true}` and
   `PUT /tournament-groups/:id {"applyToPhase":true,"autofillStrategy":"...","replaceExistingDraw":true}`.
   The padel bracket has no such control. **13.8 is retitled** to "Brackets in a
   padel tournament" and the autofill material moves to 13.2, which is the
   article about how pairs are grouped.

2. **"Football has no such tab" (13.10) and "the Configuration sub-tab"** - there
   is no Configuration sub-tab. **Configuration** is a button in the Phases
   header of the padel Format tab, and it opens a dialog titled **Padel
   Configuration**. The rest of the note holds: football has nothing like it.

3. **"A group-only tournament shows none of this" (13.11)** - right about the
   **Results** tab, wrong about the **Format** tab. A one-phase Round Robin
   tournament shows the full Phases board with **Edit Group Phase** *and*
   **Delete Group Phase**; what it has no trace of is the phase banner and the
   phase tabs on Results. The reason is not the phase count as such: the banner
   only appears when there is something to do - a next phase to start, a phase to
   end, or a start to undo.

4. **Padel's phase cards carry fewer controls than football's**, which the map
   does not mention. Football: the Group Phase has **Edit**, the Knockout Phase
   has **Edit** and **Delete**. Padel: the Group Phase has neither, and the
   Knockout Phase has **Delete** only. This does not stop 13.11 being shared -
   the phase *banner* that starts, ends and undoes a phase is identical on both
   - but a padel phase cannot be renamed.

5. **Which phase can be deleted.** The 2026-08-28 note in `state/progress.md`
   records football as "Edit/Delete Group Phase, Edit Knockout Phase". It is the
   other way round. On KB 13 Cup the Group Phase offers **Edit** only and the
   Knockout Phase offers **Edit** and **Delete**; on the one-phase KB 13 League
   the Group Phase offers both. The rule is that **only the last phase carries a
   Delete control**.

6. **Football brackets are not filled with team names.** The map says "football
   fills slots by hand - Add team to slot". The **Add Team** button opens a menu
   of *positions* - 1st Group A, 1st Group B, ... 4th Group B, Bye - not of
   teams. There is a second control, a round **+** beside it, which opens a team
   search. So a football slot can hold either a position or a named team. 13.9
   documents both.

   One more thing worth knowing before reading 13.9's screenshots: **the same
   menu offers real team names once the feeding group has been played.** On
   KB 13 Cup (nothing played) it lists positions; on KB 13 Summer Cup (group
   phase complete) it lists the eight teams. 13.9 is captured on KB 13 Cup, so it
   shows the positions.

### Scores are fixed, not random

`scoreGroupPhase` hashes the two team names, sorted, into one of eight
scorelines. The same pairing therefore always draws the same result, so the
standings table photographs identically on every run and on a rebuilt fixture. A
match will not accept a score while it is Scheduled, so each one goes
`POST /matches/:id/status {"status":"Live"}` -> `PUT /matches/:id/score` ->
`POST /matches/:id/status {"status":"Finished"}`.

### The padel format save, which collection 12 could not do

Collection 12 recorded that the two-step padel Format screen "was never captured
on the wire", drove it by hand, and left its seed able only to *check* the
result. It was captured this session: it is a plain **`PUT /tournaments/:id`**,
the same call football's format save uses, carrying `padelFormat`,
`padelStandingType`, `padelScoringPoints`, `padelWin/Loss/DrawPoints`,
`padelMin/MaxPlayers`, `padelRoundCount`, `padelCourtCount` and
`padelRoundGapMinutes`. `teamIds` is empty on a first save. `scripts/seed-13.mjs`
builds both padel fixtures from nothing with no hand work, and the call is now in
`config/api.md`.

### 13.9 Setting and renaming bracket matches in a football tournament

- Persona: organiser, Owner. Role: Owner or Admin only. Fixture: **KB 13 Cup**.
- Preconditions found: a first-round slot has **two** controls. The **Add Team**
  text button opens a menu of positions - 1st Group A through 4th Group B, plus
  **Bye**. The round **+** button beside it opens an **Add Team** dialog with a
  team search. Picking a position sends `homeSource`; picking a team sends
  `homeTeamId`. Clearing a slot is `{"homeTeamId":""}`, which is what the seed
  uses to reset the fixture.
  The position menu is replaced by a list of real team names once the feeding
  group has been played, which is why this article is captured on KB 13 Cup.
- Dual capture: no.
- Steps:
  1. On **Format**, choose a first-round match and use **Add Team** on a slot.
  2. Pick the position that should feed the slot.

## Open questions

1. **The board's Group and Bracket buttons do nothing I could observe.** Each
   phase card has a **Group** and a **Bracket** button below it, and the bundle
   has `POST /tournament-groups` and `POST /tournament-brackets` behind them.
   Clicking either on KB 13 Cup fired no request and changed no state, three
   times. The **Phase** button beside them works on the first click and adds
   "Phase 3" with no dialog and no undo. Assumed for now: adding a group or a
   bracket is refused when the format already defines the structure, and the app
   fails silently. No article documents adding one. If this turns out to be a
   real bug it belongs in a ticket, not an article.
2. **What "SCORE" counts in the padel standings.** It rises with matches played
   and is not the same as PTS, and the fixture's numbers are consistent with
   games won across the round. 13.6 says "the points the pair won across its
   matches", which matches the data but was not confirmed against a spec or a
   product owner.
3. **Whether a football team can be dragged from one group into another.**
   Dragging within a group is verified. Dragging across the group boundary was
   not attempted, because the fixture would have to be repaired and the shot does
   not need it. 13.1 claims reordering inside a group only.
4. **The three padel round-runner endpoints** (`king-next-round`,
   `king-playoffs`, `padel-next-round`) belong to formats this collection does
   not cover. Probably collection 15.
5. **The number fields in Padel Configuration barely accept typing.** The caret
   is forced back to position 0 on every render, so Backspace never deletes and
   a typed digit is prepended - typing "2" over "4" leaves "42". Playwright's
   `fill()` is swallowed outright. The stepper works, but only once: a second
   ArrowDown in the same session puts the value back up. 13.10's spec presses it
   exactly once and the article tells the reader to use the stepper. This looks
   like a bug rather than a design, and it is worth a ticket.

6. **`teamSources` versus `teamIds` on a group.** Both are accepted by
   `PUT /tournament-groups/:id`, and after a **Clear** the group's `teamIds` goes
   empty while `teamSources` fills with four `Bye` entries. Sending `teamIds` on
   a cleared padel group had no effect; sending it on a football group reordered
   it. The seed therefore uses `autofillStrategy` for padel and `teamIds` for
   football. The exact precedence rule is not known.

## Determinism

Two full runs of the suite, back to back with no re-seed between them, produce
55 screenshots that are byte-identical bar a handful. Three fixes got it there,
and they are worth knowing before writing another collection's specs:

1. **A spec that mutates its fixture must put it back itself.** 13.9 fills a
   bracket slot. Relying on the seed to clear it left nine screenshots in three
   other articles differing between runs, because those articles photograph the
   same board.
2. **Clipped captures need the page scrolled to a known position first.**
   Playwright scrolls a clipped element into view, but where it lands depends on
   where the page already was. A fractional difference in device-pixel alignment
   re-renders every glyph's antialiasing - 1.5% of the pixels on the padel
   bracket board - which changes the file's hash and therefore its URL. `shot()`
   now scrolls to the top before any clipped capture.
3. **The Results tab paints its standings table before its fixture list.** A
   capture taken as soon as the table is readable catches a half-built list in
   the bottom third of the frame. `fixturesReady()` gates on a match card's own
   status chip rather than on the FIXTURES heading, which is there before the
   list has anything in it.

What is left is a small residue: one or two captures per pair of runs differ by
a fraction of a percent of their pixels, from font antialiasing. The repo owner's
call, made during this run, was **not to chase it** - the images are the same
image, and a new content hash on a re-run costs nothing but a new URL.
