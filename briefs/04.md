"Pro is free" is true of the first and false of the second. Every article here

### Why this collection uses three accounts and not one flipped

`config/personas.yaml` says, under `manager_free`: "The Free/Pro pairs in
collections 04 and 18 are captured by flipping THIS account with
change-user-membership between the two captures ... Flip back to Free when done."

**This collection does not do that**, and the note is out of date. The
"Actions you can only do once" section of `docs/style-guide.md` says the opposite
and is right:

> A `free_pro` article gets two seeded accounts, one Free and one Pro. Never one
> account flipped between captures: the specs then only work in one order.

A flipped account fails in two ordinary ways here. 04.3's six captures are all
gates that vanish the moment the account is Pro, so any spec that leaves the flip
half-done destroys them; and a crash between the flip and the flip back leaves the
persona Pro for every later run, silently, with the specs still passing on the
articles that do not care. So:

- `kb-manager-free-04@` is Free and is **never** upgraded;
- `kb-04-pro@` is Pro and is **never** downgraded;
- `kb-04-upgrade@` is the only account 04.2 touches, and its spec resets it in a
  `finally`.

`personas.yaml` should be corrected for 18 as well. Not done here - 18 is not this
session's collection.

Ten things were observed that `config/api.md` did not say. All ten are now in it,
marked `(observed in app, 2026-08-29)`, most of them in a new section,
"Membership, plans and the Free-plan limits":

1. **`/subscriptions` has two tabs and they are different products.** Platform Pro
   is free during the beta; Tournament Pro is GBP 19.99 / EUR 22.99 / USD per
   tournament, or GBP 215 annual.
2. **The plan cards are not served by the Scoryboard API.** They come from
   `GET /api/prismic/subscription-plans` on the app host, out of Prismic, so the
   copy can change with no deploy. Pinned in `lib/fixtures-04.mjs`.
3. **Neither direction is confirmed.** "Upgrade to PRO" upgrades on the click;
   "Cancel subscription" downgrades on the click. No dialog either way.
4. **The complete `MembershipLimits` enum** - eight error codes, with what raises
   each and which modal it opens.
5. **The modal never shows the API's wording.** It is keyed off the error code and
   renders the app's own i18n string. The API says "upgrade to Pro", the modal
   says "upgrade to **Pro Membership**".
6. **`GET /friends` excludes a friend who has joined one of your teams**, and the
   Free limit of 14 is measured against that filtered list. Adding a friend to a
   team therefore frees a slot.
7. **`POST /team-players` with a `name` creates a friend record**, so on a full
   list it answers `FRIEND_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` and no team limit is ever reached.
   `playerId` (the friend's `friendPlayerId`) and `name` are mutually exclusive.
8. **There is no team-count limit on Free.**
9. **A new account is born with one leaderboard and two teams**, so the
   leaderboard limit is reached the moment the account exists.
10. **`GET /players/:playerId` as somebody else records a profile view**, deduped
    by viewer. It is the only way found to seed one, and the counter is a control
    only on your own profile and only while the count is above zero.

One earlier note was **qualified**, not overturned. `config/api.md` records, from
collection 07, that "Select player from friend list" never lists anybody on an
account with 19 friends. On this account, with 14, it lists all fourteen and
picking one works. Both observations stand and the trigger is unknown; the api.md
note now says so.

### 04.3 Every Free-plan limit, and the messages you will see

- Persona: **Marc (Free)** for shots 01-06, **Nia (Pro)** for 07-08.
- Preconditions found: five of the six refusals need the account sitting exactly
  ON a limit, not near it. The seed does that. Three findings shaped how the
  captures are reached:
  - **The friend refusal arrives on the way in.** With the list already at 14,
    selecting Add Friend never opens the dialog - the gate opens instead. (A
    stale page that still believes the list is at 13 opens the form and is
    refused when it posts. Same modal, one step later. The article documents the
    first.)
  - **The team-admin refusal has to be reached from the friends list.** Its
    "Add To Team" control is the one path that sends an existing friend's id.
    Typing a name into the Edit Team page's Add New Player creates a friend
    record as a side effect, so on a full list that answers the FRIEND limit and
    you never reach the admin one.
  - **The Views counter is only a control while the count is above zero**, and at
    1440 wide the clickable target is the Views tile in the counters row. The
    small "N views" button beside the rating is the narrow-layout copy and has a
    zero-sized box; clicking it waits for ever. The ratings control next to it
    reads "0 reviews", which contains "views" - an easy mis-hit.
- Dual capture: **yes**, and it is the last two shots rather than a mirror of the
  first six. The Pro counterpart of a gate is usually "no modal appears", which is
  not a screenshot. So the two Pro captures are the two places where something
  different actually happens: Create New Leaderboard opens the form, and the Views
  counter opens the list of viewers.
- Steps:
  1. **Friends.** Free stops at 14. Selecting Add Friend on a full list opens
     Friend Limit Reached.
  2. **Team administrators.** Add a friend to a team with the role Administrator
     and Scoryboard answers Team Limit Reached. Owner is the only role Free gets.
  3. **Leaderboards.** Free owns one, and every account is born with it, so
     Create New Leaderboard is refused from the start.
  4. **Leaderboard administrators.** Same answer, its own message.
  5. **Compare.** On somebody else's profile, Compare opens Unlock Compare
     with Pro.
  6. **Profile views.** On your own profile the Views counter opens Unlock
     Profile Views. The count is visible on Free; the list of who is not.
  7. On Pro the same Create New Leaderboard button opens the form.
  8. On Pro the same Views counter opens Profile Views, the list of viewers.

## Open questions

1. **`ONE_FRIEND_PER_TEAM` cannot be reached through the app.** The API returns it
   reliably - `POST /team-players` with the `friendPlayerId` of somebody already
   on one of your teams. But no screen can produce it. A friend who joins a team
   leaves `GET /friends`, and both controls that could offer them again - the
   friends list and "Select player from friend list" - read that same list. So
   the code exists, the message exists, and nothing in the UI raises it.
   **Assumed in the meantime:** the article states the limit in its table with the
   modal's wording and no screenshot, and does not claim the reader can trigger
   it. Worth a ticket: either the filter is wrong or the message is dead code.
2. **Whether `USER_FREE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` and `FRIEND_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` are meant to
   be two codes.** They differ only in which endpoint raises them and they open
   the same modal with the same message. Documented as one limit.
3. **"Select player from friend list" works here and did not in collection 07.**
   14 friends against 19. The trigger is unknown. Both observations are now in
   `config/api.md`; neither article depends on the control.
4. **What the substitute limit actually is.** The bundle's lineup code tests
   `3 >= parseInt(...)` against a `Substitute-N` slot name, which reads as three
   substitutes on Free. Not confirmed against a real lineup - see "Unreachable".
   The table says 3 and this question says it is unverified.
5. **Adverts.** The BASIC card says "Ads enabled" and the PRO card "Ads Free".
   Nothing in the staging app renders an advert, so what a Free reader actually
   sees is unknown. The table repeats the plan card's wording and nothing more.
6. **Cancel subscription during the beta.** It downgrades immediately and says
   nothing. Whether anything is retained, or whether re-upgrading is unlimited,
   was not tested beyond doing it twice on the throwaway account, which worked.

## Unreachable

| Limit | Why not |
|---|---|
| Reading incoming chat in full | Needs an unread incoming message from a second account. Collection 18 owns chat and should photograph this. |

| Photos and video on a match feed | Needs a finished match with a comment feed. Collection 19. |
| Adverts | Nothing in staging renders one. |

`ONE_FRIEND_PER_TEAM` is a fifth, and a different kind - see open question 1. It
is not unreachable because of fixture cost; it appears to be unreachable
full stop.
