## The two things that decide this whole collection

**1. Nothing works without a connected payout account.** `Request Payment` on an
account without one does not open a form. It fires `POST /payments/stripe/account`,
then `POST /payments/stripe/account-session`, and renders Stripe's
`stripe-connect-account-onboarding` component in a Scoryboard dialog. At the API,
`POST /payments` answers `500 internal error` for any account whose payout account
is not live - verified against four accounts in three different states. There is no
admin endpoint that enables one.

**2. Scoryboard hands off to Stripe twice, and this collection stops at both.**

| Handoff | What fires | What opens |
|---|---|---|
| Setting up a payout account | `POST /payments/stripe/account-session` | **Add information** opens a **new browser window** at `connect.stripe.com` |
| Paying a request | `POST /payments/transaction/:id/pay` | **Stripe Elements** in the page - card number, expiry, security code, country, plus Google Pay |

On instruction for this run, **no capture crosses either boundary**. The last shot
in 17.2 is the Scoryboard screen that leads into Stripe; the last shot in 17.8 is
**Pay Now** before it is pressed. What is on the other side is described in prose:
"Stripe's form opens - follow its instructions to finish."

This matches what collection 04 did with Tournament Pro, where "every capture stops
before the payment gateway" (`briefs/04.md`).

**Both handoffs are CAPTCHA-gated**, which is also why they could not be automated:
Stripe's signup raises a visible hCaptcha challenge on submit, and the payment form
loads `hcaptcha-invisible`. The payout account on `kb-manager-pro-17@` was
connected by a human, once, on 2026-09-01.

### Why the cancelled request is seeded and not made by a spec

`DELETE /payments/:id` does not delete. It sets the status to `Cancelled` and the
row stays for ever, visible in the requester's table. A spec that cancelled one of
the others would add a dead row on every run and change what 17.7 photographs.
`KB 17 Away travel` is cancelled once, by the seed, and every later run finds it by
title and leaves it. This is `docs/style-guide.md`, "Actions you can only do once":
take the after state from a second fixture.

### The clock

`FROZEN_NOW = 2026-09-05T09:00:00.000Z`, before the fixtures' due date of
**30 September 2026**. That matters for more than the calendar's Today marker: the
app computes `overdue` in the browser as `dueDate <= now`, and an overdue request
has **Add participants** and **Edit** disabled. Without the freeze, 17.6's captures
would change behaviour the day the due date passes.

### 17.5 Fees - passing them on or absorbing them

- Persona: **manager_pro** for the form, **player** for what the payer is charged.
- Preconditions found: the fee is computed in the browser, not quoted by any endpoint. `total = ceil((base + 2% + 20p) / 0.985)`. At a £10 base that is a 56p fee and a £10.56 total. Confirmed against the app to the penny.
- **Pass fee to users** is **on by default**. With it on the form shows a breakdown - You will receive / Transaction fee / Total price. With it off the breakdown **disappears entirely**, so the organiser is never told what they will actually receive.
- Dual capture: no, but two states of the same toggle.
- Steps:
  1. Set a base price. The fee appears underneath.
  2. **Pass fee to users** is on. Your players pay the total and you receive the base price.
  3. Turn it off to absorb the fee yourself. Your players pay the base price and the fee comes out of your payout.
  4. Check what your players will actually be charged on their **PAY** list.

### 17.9 Payment statuses and failed payments

- Persona: **manager_pro** and **player**.
- **RETITLED.** The map said "Payment statuses, failed payments and refunds". The app has no refund feature - `refund` appears zero times in the bundle, there is no endpoint, and there is no control. The article says a refund is a support request and does not pretend otherwise.
- Preconditions found: two enums, and they are not the same.

  2. Open a request to see where each person is.
  3. **Paid** is green, **Failed** is red. A failed payment can be tried again.
  4. **Cancelled** means the request was stopped. Nobody can pay it now.
  5. To get money back to somebody, contact support.

## Open questions

1. **Is `Processing` reachable at all?** It is in the enum and has a colour, but no
   observed response ever carried it. Assumed to be the window between Stripe
   accepting a card and the webhook landing. Documented from the enum, not seen.
2. **A due date in the past can be chosen.** The picker does not disable earlier
   days - 31 August was selectable on 1 September. Not documented, because a
   request created that way was never sent and the consequence is unknown.
3. **Does a reminder do anything visible to the person reminded?** `POST
   /payments/:id/reminders` answers 200 and the app says "Reminder sent
   successfully." Nothing was checked in Pia's inbox or notifications. 17.6 says a
   reminder is sent and does not claim what it looks like at the other end.
4. **`POST /payments/transaction/:id/verify` was never called.** It is in the
   bundle and presumably runs after Stripe returns. Not documented.

## Worth a look, and worth a ticket

1. **"You're all set to receive payments!" is shown when the account is not set
   up.** The dialog fires when the Stripe window closes, not when the account
   becomes chargeable. Reproduced twice on 2026-09-01: an account left `Restricted`
   with `chargesEnabled: false` got the same congratulation as a completed one.
   This is why 17.2 tells the reader to check by pressing **Request Payment**
   again rather than trusting the message.
2. **Select Team and Select Leaderboard are empty until `/teams` is visited.**
   "Your Teams (0) / No teams found where you are the owner" for an owner of two
   teams. Same persisted-Redux cause as the External badge. A reader who goes
   straight to Payment after signing in will hit it.
3. **Absorbing the fee hides the breakdown.** With **Pass fee to users** off, the
   organiser is shown no figure at all for what they will receive - and the helper
   text underneath still reads "Recipients will pay the total price and will not
   see the transaction fee", which is the opposite of what is now happening.
4. **The Cancel Request window has two buttons both labelled some form of
   "Cancel".** "Cancel" dismisses, "Cancel request" confirms.
5. **A cancelled request's people still read Pending.** The status is overridden on
   the request row but not on the participant rows.

## Deviations from the plan above

1. **17.3's shot 08 shows the match panel's Requests sub-tab, not the Pay one.**
   The panel opens on Pay, which for the owner reads "No payments found." and says
   nothing about requesting. The Requests tab has the match's own request on it.
2. **17.4's shot 05 is a viewport capture, not a clipped one.** See defect 3.
3. **17.2's shot 03 contains a spinner, deliberately.** `docs/style-guide.md` says a
   spinner must not appear, and that rule is there to stop a capture landing
   mid-load. Here the loading state *is* the subject: it is the last thing
   Scoryboard shows before a Stripe window opens over the top, and a reader about
   to be surprised by a new window is the person the shot is for. The spec holds
   `POST /payments/stripe/account` open so the state is captured deliberately
   rather than caught by accident. **Flagged for the reviewer** - if they disagree,
   drop the shot and 17.2 stands at three.
