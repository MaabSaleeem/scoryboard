# Collection 05 - Friends

Persona default: manager_free   Feature flag: none
Articles in map: 4   Articles in brief: 4   Screenshots planned: 20

The friends list is a private list of people you have added. Most of them are
placeholders you typed in yourself; some stand for real Scoryboard accounts. It
is the pool every "add a player to my team" control reads from, which is why the
map calls 05.1 the highest ticket driver in this area.

Four things about it shape every article here, and all four were found on
staging on 2026-08-30:

1. **A refused Add To Team deletes the friend.** On Free a friend may be on one
   of your teams only. Asking for a second answers `400 ONE_FRIEND_PER_TEAM`,
   raises a **Team Limit Reached** modal, **and soft-deletes the friend record**.
   The person stays on the team they were already on and vanishes from your
   friends list. Reproduced from the API and through the app.
2. **A friend row that stands for a real account cannot be edited.** Its Edit
   menu item is `aria-disabled`, its Chat button is live, and
   `GET /friends/:id/shareCode` refuses it. A placeholder is the opposite on all
   three.
3. **Friendship is one-way.** Somebody who accepts your invitation joins *your*
   list. Nothing lands on theirs.
4. **A malformed email stops the Add Friend form with no message.** The button
   does nothing and no request is sent. The empty-name case does show
   "This field is required."

### Why the two born teams are deleted

Every account is born with `<First> K FC` and `<First> K FC Away`, and those
names gain a **date** suffix when they clash - this account's read
`Marc K FC 3008`. 05.1 photographs the Select Team dropdown, so a date in a team
name would be a date in a screenshot. The seed deletes every team the account
owns that is not one of the two fixed ones. Free has no team-count limit, so
this costs nothing.

## What the run changed in this brief - 2026-08-30

Step 2 ran straight after step 1, as the workflow requires. Everything below was
found while capturing and is corrected here rather than left in the report alone.

### The claimer account moved, and why that is not a detail

`kb-05-claimer@yopmail.com` is **burnt** and this collection now uses
`kb-05-invitee@yopmail.com` instead.

The first version of the seed repaired an account carrying the wrong name by
deleting it and creating it again. That does not give a clean account.
`DELETE /admins/user-delete/:id` leaves the **player** behind, anonymised - its
name blanked and its address rewritten to `<userId>@scoryboard.com` - and an
account created again at the same email is handed that same playerId back. Every
friend record that ever pointed at it is still there, soft-deleted.

`POST /friends/join/:shareCode` then revives one of those old records **instead
of** taking over the record whose link was used. 05.4 produced a friends list
with a nameless row on it and its placeholder gone.

Both behaviours are now in `config/api.md`. `scripts/seed-05.mjs` no longer
deletes an account to fix its name: it stops and says to use a new address.
**Any collection whose seed offers `--rebuild` should read this** - a rebuilt
account is not a new account.

### Three capture decisions

- **05.3/04 and 05.4/05 are clipped with no pad.** The 12px pad those two rows
  were planned with caught a sliver of the rows above and below. The pad exists
  to keep a dimmed page out of a modal's rounded corners, and neither of these
  sits over a dimmed page.
- **05.4/04 has its animation removed before the capture.** The accepted-
  invitation dialog puts a 192px Lottie animation above its message. Lottie draws
  from JavaScript, so neither `animations: 'disabled'` nor
  `reducedMotion: 'reduce'` settles it, and there is no end frame to wait on -
  the capture came back as two blue dots in a large white square. `hideLottie()`
  in `lib/kb.ts` removes the box and the dialog collapses to its heading, its
  message and Close. The article does not mention the animation.
- **Nothing in 05.4 needed masking.** The plan was to show the invitation link
  because it is the article's subject. In the event the field is narrower than
  the address and shows only `https://staging-sb.app.scoryboard.c` - no share
  code is legible in either capture. 05.4 tells the reader to use **Copy link**
  for that reason.

### Two captures that look alike

05.4/01 and 05.4/02 are the same panel, because the app opens the same panel from
both places. Only the dialog heading differs - **Add Friend** against **Edit
Friend** - and that heading is in frame on both. The article's table and its step
text carry the difference. Worth a reviewer's eye: if it reads as a duplicate,
the fix is a product one, not a capture one.

arrives from `GET /friends/invite-code`.
