### What the `role` flag actually means here

Every article except 12.1 carries `role`. On a tournament the roles are Owner,
Admin, and everyone else. Two facts cover all nine articles, so each one states
them in its first two lines rather than carrying its own role screenshots:

- **Owner and Admin see the same board**, at `/tournaments/<id>/<tab>`: nine tabs
  and the settings page. Verified by signing in as `kb-12-admin@yopmail.com` and
  opening both.
- **Everyone else is redirected** to the read-only Info view: six tabs, no
  settings, no editing. Verified as `kb-12-outsider@yopmail.com`.

The one screenshot of the difference lives in 12.8 (`06`). Repeating it in the
other eight would be eight near-identical images.

### Two fixtures, and it has to stay two

Creating a tournament spends one of the organiser's free Tournament Pro slots,
and **the slot cannot be given back** - the grant endpoint is additive and there
is no revoke. So no spec creates a tournament. Every "creating" article fills the
Create Tournament modal in and then closes it, and the "and here is what you land
on" shot comes from KB New Cup, which is exactly that state. The same rule
applies to Add Team, Add Owner, Add Admin, Add Referee and Delete: opened,
filled, captured, dismissed.

This is also why `KB Draft Cup` and `KB Live Cup`, created during exploration,
were deleted again (`DELETE /tournaments/<id>`), and why the eight global teams
seeded before I learned that tournament teams are separate entities were deleted
too.

### 12.4 Choosing a format for a football tournament

- Persona: organiser. Role state: Owner or Admin.
- Preconditions found: the **Encounters** field stays empty until both the group
  count and the teams-per-group are set. Saving the format is what generates the
  groups and the fixture list - it is not a draft save.
- What the three templates actually ask for, verified on screen:
  - **Group phase only** - number of groups, teams per group, Encounters, and
    whether overflow matches roll to the next day.
  - **Knockout phase only** - one question, "How many teams proceed to
    knockout?", asked with a picker.
  - **Group & knockout** - both sets.
- Validation rules for "If it does not work", read off the app's own schema:
  - The team count must divide by the group count. The app names the divisors
    that would work.
  - Teams per group must equal team count ÷ group count. The app says which.
  - Ranges: groups 1-64, teams per group 2-64, matches per team 1-128.
  - The schema also caps knockout entrants at a power of two and the knockout-only
    team count at 2/4/8/16/32/64. **Those two messages may be unreachable**: both
    counts are chosen from a picker, not typed, so there is nothing invalid to
    enter. Do not write them up until someone has seen them fire.

## What the exploration changed in `config/api.md`

Recorded with the date, marked `(observed in app, 2026-08-28)`:

- `POST /admins/generate-signin-token` returns a **sign-in URL**, not a token. The
  Firebase custom token is inside the `token` query parameter's JWT payload, under
  `customLoginToken`.
- `POST /admins/users/tournament-free-pro/grant` is **additive**. Two calls of
  `quantity: 2` leave 4. There is no revoke.
- `GET /tournaments/:id/config` returns **404**. The `PATCH .../config` row in the
  Postman export is not what the app calls either; the app `PUT`s the tournament.
- New rows for `GET /tournaments`, `GET /tournaments/:id`, `POST /tournaments`
  (real body), `PUT /tournaments/:id`, `POST /tournaments/:id/teams/bulk`,
  `GET /tournaments/:id/participants`, `POST` and `DELETE /tournaments/:id/admin`,
  `POST /tournaments/:id/referee`, `DELETE /tournaments/:id/referee/:playerId`,
  `DELETE /tournaments/:id`, `GET /tournaments/:id/follow`,
  `GET /tournaments/:id/chat/settings`, `GET /tournament-phases`,
  `GET /tournament-groups`.
- `GET /club-locations`: the `isTournament` and `saveForFutureTournaments` flags,
  and the fact that the two listings are disjoint.
- `GET /teams/:id/players` returns removed members with `isDeleted: true`.
- The singular/plural route split: `/tournaments/:id/*` is the organiser board,
  `/tournament/:id/*` is the public page. Both were seen serving the read-only
  Info view to a user with no role, so the specs assert the tab, not the path.

## The specs have been run

Not as a capture for publication - those come in step 2 - but as a check that the
specs actually work before a human is asked to approve them.

- **11 tests, 11 passing, 65 screenshots** - exactly the number in the table above.
- **Run twice from a clean directory, all 65 files byte-identical.** That is the
  determinism bar in `docs/workflow.md`, and it is met.

Getting there turned up five things worth knowing before step 2 runs these:

1. **Images are not waited for.** Playwright's screenshot fires before remote
   images paint, and the tournament header is a remote photo - eight captures
   came out with a black banner on one run and the photo on the next.
   `imagesPainted()` in `lib/kb.ts` now blocks on every `<img>` and every CSS
   background image, and `shot()` calls it before every capture.
2. **Half these screens render twice.** A wide-layout copy and a narrow-layout
   copy of the same control, with a zero-sized box on the unused one. A plain
   match is ambiguous and `.first()` can pick the invisible twin. `onScreen()`
   narrows to the copy on screen.
3. **Section headings are upper-cased by CSS.** The DOM says "Profile
   Appearance"; the screen says "PROFILE APPEARANCE". Playwright matches the DOM.
   Every heading match in these specs is case-insensitive.
4. **The rename pencils sit outside the accessibility tree.** They carry
   `aria-label="Edit Group Phase"` but `getByRole` never finds them, so 12.7
   matches the attribute directly.
5. **The venue picker's add button dismisses its own popover** under a real
   mouse click, so the form never opens. 12.3 and 12.6 dispatch the click on the
   element instead. This is the one place a spec leans on a styling class
   (`.rounded-full.cursor-pointer`) because the control has no accessible name -
   if it is restyled, those two specs break, and that is the first place to look.

The captures from those validation runs were deleted. `screenshots/12/` is empty,
so step 2 starts clean.

## Game types: what these articles cover

**Added 2026-08-28, after publication, at the repo owner's request.**

The Create Tournament form offers three game types, and they are not equivalent.
Verified in the rendered form, not just the bundle:

| Game type | Create form | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Football | name, game type, start date, start time, match duration, club | three templates: group only, knockout only, group & knockout |
| Other Sports | **identical to Football** | not verified; the bundle has only one non-Padel format set, so presumed the same three |
| Padel | Football's fields **plus** Play mode, Enrollment type, and a Friendly Tournament toggle | its own set, with its own questions about players, courts and scoring |

So the real divide is Padel against everything else, not football against
everything else. Two articles were retitled to name their scope, because the
screen itself differs by game type:

- 12.2 -> "Creating a football tournament"
- 12.4 -> "Choosing a format for a football tournament"

Both carry a line saying Other Sports behaves identically and Padel is covered
separately, so an Other Sports reader is not sent away from an article that does
in fact describe their screen.

Two more got a pointer rather than a retitle, because their flow is not itself
game-type specific:

- 12.5 - a tournament entered by teams is how Football and Other Sports work;
  Padel can be entered by individual players. The form's own helper text says
  "Email is optional when adding Padel players", which is the evidence.
- 12.7 - the crest and banner are the same on every tournament; what appears on
  the Format tab follows the format you chose.

Left alone deliberately: 12.1, 12.3, 12.6, 12.8, 12.9 and 12.10. Tournament
states, dates, venues, settings, admins, referees and deletion are the same

## Production was checked against staging

The three mismatches below are specific enough that someone clearly wrote the map
while looking at something. The obvious candidate was production being ahead of
staging, which would have made this whole collection wrong. It is not.

`app.scoryboard.com` serves its JavaScript publicly. Swept it the same way as
staging (read-only, no account, no credentials) and compared the markers:

| Marker | Production | Staging |
|---|---|---|
| `"Draft"` as a tournament status | absent | absent |
| `registrationFee` / `entryFee` / `refereeFee` | absent | absent |
| `tournamentSetupStep1Schema` | present | present |
| `"Continue Setup"` / `"Finish Setup"` / `"Format & Group Setup"` | present | present |
| `"Choose Format"` | present | present |
| `"Edit Tournament"` | present | present |
| `"Only tournament owners and administrators..."` | present | present |

The 17 chunk files the two builds have in common are byte-identical.

Two conclusions worth carrying into step 2:

1. **Staging is not behind.** What these articles document is what a production
   user sees today.
2. **The setup-wizard strings are dead code in production too**, not a staging
   gap. Whatever that wizard was, it is not shipped to anyone. So the retitles in
   12.2, 12.3 and 12.4 are not a workaround for a stale environment - they are
   what the product is.

That leaves the map itself as the thing written against something else. Worth
asking the client where it came from, because the same source probably fed the
other 23 collections.

## Open questions

1. **There is no Draft state, in either environment.** A tournament is created
   with `status: "Published"`, and the string `Draft` does not appear in the
   staging or the production bundle. The statuses the code compares against are Published, Live,
   Completed and Cancelled. *Assumed in the meantime:* 12.1 documents the real
   distinction a reader can see - format chosen or not chosen - and names
   Published, Live and Completed. Should the article name Cancelled too? I never
   produced one.

2. **There is no numbered setup wizard.** 12.3 and 12.4 are titled "Step 1" and
   "Step 2". The app has a Create Tournament modal and then a Format tab, with no
   step numbers and no next/back. The app's own code does carry
   `tournamentSetupStep1Schema` and `tournamentSetupStep2Schema`, and the strings
   "Continue Setup", "Finish Setup" and "Format & Group Setup" exist in the
   bundle - **and in the production bundle too**, with no way to reach it in
   either. So it is unreleased or behind a server flag, not a staging gap.
   *Assumed in the meantime:* documented the two screens in the order a reader
   meets them, without step numbers. **Worth asking the client whether the
   stepped wizard is coming, because three article titles assume it.**

3. **Registration fees and referee fees do not exist**, in staging or in
   production. No fee field on the create modal, the format tab or the settings
   page, and no matching string in either bundle. The only fee copy in the app belongs to payment requests
   (collection 17). *Assumed in the meantime:* 12.6 covers venues only, and says
   nothing about money.

4. ~~**There is no tournament banner.**~~ **Answered, and the brief was wrong.**
   There is one, next to the crest under PROFILE APPEARANCE, with its own edit
   and delete controls. 12.7 covers both. The lesson generalises: reading a page
   as text misses every control that carries no text. The route sweep in step 1
   should be paired with a look at the rendered page, not just its copy.

5. **"Access Denied" is copy that no state reached.** The bundle carries "Access
   Denied" / "Only tournament owners and administrators can access tournament
   settings." What actually happens to someone with no role is a silent redirect
   to `/tournament/<id>/info`. *Assumed in the meantime:* 12.8 documents the
   redirect and does not show an Access Denied screen. If that screen is meant to
   render, this is a bug worth raising rather than documenting.

6. **The Format tab's own note points the wrong way.** It reads "Note: You can
   change the formation later from Settings", but the settings page has no format
   control in this build - the format is changed back on the Format tab.
   *Assumed in the meantime:* 12.10 says the format is changed on the Format tab,
   and does not repeat the note.

7. **Settings has two entrances, neither of them a tab.** From the tournament
   list, the card's three-dot menu: **Edit** opens settings, **Remove** deletes.
   From the board, a small gear badge on the tournament crest. There is no
   settings tab in the tab bar, which is where a reader will look first.
   *Assumed in the meantime:* 12.8 shows the card menu as step one and names the
   gear as the alternative; 12.10 mentions Remove alongside the Danger Zone
   delete. The gear is small and unlabelled - worth confirming it is the intended
   affordance.

8. **Two supporting accounts are not in `personas.yaml`.**
   `kb-12-admin@yopmail.com` and `kb-12-outsider@yopmail.com`. Promote them, or
   leave them local to this collection?

9. **A load race that will bite step 2.** Several screens render their empty
   state first and fill in when the fetch returns - the tournament list shows
   "Your Tournaments (0)", and the settings page redirects a user whose role has
   not loaded yet. Every spec here waits on settled content, never on a URL and
   never on a duration. Flagging it because it cost me a wrong conclusion during
   exploration, twice.

## Unreachable

- **The live lock (12.10).** The bundle carries "This tournament is already live,
  so the name and dates are locked. You can still update the rest of the settings
  below." I could not produce a Live tournament.
  `PUT /tournaments/:id {"status":"Live"}` answers `200` and changes nothing;
  before a format is saved it answers `400 "Tournament config is incomplete"`.
  Live appears to be reached by starting a match, which is collection 15.
  **12.10 is written and captured without that shot** - five of its six
  screenshots are the editing and deletion flow, which is fully reachable. The
  live-lock paragraph is left out rather than guessed. Suggest revisiting it from
  collection 15, when a Live tournament exists.

  question 3.

  question 5.
