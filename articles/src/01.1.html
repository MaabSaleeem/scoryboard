You need an email address and a password. Signing up takes two screens, and ends with a six-digit code sent to your inbox.

Create your account

  1. Open Scoryboard. Signed out, it shows Create your account. {{shot:01|The Create your account form: an Email field, a Password field, a Sign up button, and Sign up with Google and Sign up with Apple below it.}}
  2. Enter your email address and a password. The password must be at least six characters. {{shot:02|The form with an address and a password entered, and the Sign up button outlined.}}
  3. Select Sign up. Scoryboard opens Personal information. {{shot:03|The Personal information step, headed Step 1, with First name, Last name, Email, Gender, Sports and Preferred position.}}
  4. Fill in your first name, last name, gender, sport and preferred position. All five are required. Tick the box to accept the Terms of Conditions and the Privacy Policy. The second box, about early access, is optional. {{shot:04|Personal information filled in, the terms box ticked, and the Continue button outlined.}}
  5. Select Continue. Scoryboard emails you a six-digit code. {{shot:05|The Email Verification screen with six empty boxes and a Verify email button.}}
  6. Read the code from your inbox and type it into the six boxes. Scoryboard checks it as soon as you type the sixth digit.

Signing up with Google or Apple

Select Sign up with Google and follow Google's prompts. Select Sign up with Apple and follow Apple's prompts.

Either way you come back to Personal information and carry on from step 4.

What you will see

The verification screen shows the address the code went to. The Verify email button stays greyed out until all six digits are in.

Your email address is fixed. You can change your name, your sport and your position later in Profile settings, but not the address you signed up with.

If it does not work

  • The email has not arrived. Wait for the countdown under the boxes to reach zero. It is then replaced by Resend. Check your spam folder before you resend. {{shot:06|The verification screen after the countdown, with the Resend link outlined.}}
  • "Password must be at least 6 characters". Your password is too short. Six characters is the minimum. {{shot:07|The sign-up form showing Password must be at least 6 characters under the password field.}}
  • "This email is already in use. Please use a different one." You already have an account with that address. Sign in instead, or reset your password. {{shot:08|The sign-up form showing This email is already in use. Please use a different one.}}
  • "Email must be valid". Check the address for a typo.
