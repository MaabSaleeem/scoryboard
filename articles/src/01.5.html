You already own two teams. Scoryboard makes them when your account is created, named after you. Add your own team when you want one with a real name and the right team size.

Add a team

  1. Open Teams. Your two starter teams are already listed. {{shot:01|Manage Teams listing two teams named after the account, with the Add Team button outlined.}}
  2. Select Add Team. {{shot:02|The Add Team dialog: Upload team's Avatar, Team name, Team size, two checkboxes and an Add button.}}
  3. Enter a Team name. It must be one nobody else already owns.
  4. Choose a Team size. There is no default - the 5 VS 5 you can see is placeholder text, and you have to pick a size yourself. {{shot:03|The Team size list open over the dialog, showing 5 VS 5 through to 10 VS 10.}}
  5. Select Add. {{shot:04|The Add Team dialog with a name entered, 7 VS 7 chosen, and the Add button outlined.}}
  6. Your team appears in the list, with you as Owner. {{shot:05|Manage Teams with the new team added as a third row, showing 7 VS 7 and Owner.}}

Create your leaderboard

  1. Open Leaderboards. {{shot:06|The Leaderboards screen reading Your Leaderboards (0) and No leaderboard found, with Create New Leaderboard outlined.}}
  2. Select Create New Leaderboard, then enter a Leaderboard name. {{shot:07|The Create Leaderboard dialog with a name entered and the Add button outlined.}}
  3. Select Add. Your leaderboard appears, with you as Owner. {{shot:08|The Leaderboards screen reading Your Leaderboards (1), with the new leaderboard card and an Open Board button.}}

What you will see

A new team has no matches and one member: you. Add the rest from the team's own screen.

A new leaderboard is empty until you add teams to it and play a match.

If it does not work

  • "This field is required." under Team size. You have not chosen a size. The 5 VS 5 on the closed field is placeholder text, not a choice.
  • The team name is refused. Team names must be unique, and somebody else may own that one. Try a different name.
  • "Leaderboard Limit Reached". A Free account may have one leaderboard. Delete the one you have, or upgrade. See "Every Free-plan limit, and the messages you will see".
