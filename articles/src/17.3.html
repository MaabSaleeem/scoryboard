Every payment request starts by choosing who to charge. Scoryboard offers three lists to choose them from.

Before you start

You need a payout account. See {{link:17.2|Setting up your payout account}}.

On a team, a leaderboard or a match, only the Owner can ask for money. An Administrator and a Player see the PAYMENT tab, but only its Pay half.

Choosing a source

  1. Open Payment and choose Request Payment. {{shot:01|The Payment page with the Request Payment button outlined.}}
  2. Pick where your players come from. {{shot:02|The Request Payment window offering Leaderboards, Teams and Friends.}}
  3. Teams lists the teams you own. {{shot:03|The Select Team window reading "Your Teams (2)", with KB 17 United outlined.}}
  4. Leaderboards lists the leaderboards you own. {{shot:04|The Select Leaderboard window reading "Your Leaderboards (1)".}}
  5. Friends lists everybody on your friends list. {{shot:05|The Select Friends window listing nine friends.}}

Starting from a team, a leaderboard or a match

  1. Open the team and choose its PAYMENT tab. As the Owner you get Requests, Pay and a Request Payment button. {{shot:06|The PAYMENT tab on a team page as the Owner, with Request Payment outlined.}}
  2. Somebody who does not own the team sees the same tab with only Pay on it. {{shot:07|The same PAYMENT tab as a team Administrator, showing only the Pay control.}}
  3. A match has its own PAYMENT panel, and a request raised there belongs to that match. {{shot:08|The PAYMENT panel on a match page, with Request Payment outlined and one request listed.}}

If it does not work

  • Select Team says "No teams found where you are the owner". Open Teams in the sidebar once, then try again. Scoryboard reads your teams when you visit that page. {{shot:09|The Select Team window reading "Your Teams (0)" and "No teams found where you are the owner."}}
  • There is no Request Payment button. You do not own that team or leaderboard. Only the Owner can ask for money on it.
  • Choosing Request Payment opens a Stripe window. Your payout account is not finished. See {{link:17.2|Setting up your payout account}}.
