Your home page is your own player profile. The sidebar down the left is the same on every screen in Scoryboard.

Find your way around

  1. Sign in. Scoryboard opens your home page. {{shot:01|The Scoryboard home page: the sidebar on the left, and the signed-in player's banner, photo, counters, bio and statistics on the right.}}
  2. Read the sidebar. Home brings you back here. Leaderboards, Tournament, Teams, Friends, Chat, Payment, Schedule and Subscriptions each open one part of Scoryboard. {{shot:02|The sidebar with the navigation list outlined: Home, Leaderboards, Tournament, Teams, Friends, Chat, Payment, Schedule and Subscriptions.}}
  3. Use the search box at the top of the sidebar to find anybody on Scoryboard. {{shot:03|The top of the sidebar, with the search box outlined.}}
  4. Read the strip at the top of your home page. It holds your banner, your photo, your rating and four counters: Followers, Following, Leaderboard and Views. Views counts how many people have opened your profile. Select it to see who they were, in a panel called Profile Views. That list is a Pro feature; on Free, selecting it offers the upgrade instead. {{shot:04|The profile strip: banner, photo, name, rating, and the Followers, Following, Leaderboard and Views counters. The Views number is blacked out.}}
  5. Read your statistics below it. Ten tiles: Matches, Win, Loss, Draw, W/L ratio, Player of the match, Goals scored, Assists and a tile for each card colour. {{shot:05|The ten statistic tiles, showing 1 match, 1 win, 1 Player of the match and 2 goals scored.}}
  6. Scroll to Trending for recent activity from across Scoryboard: teams created, matches finished, people following each other. {{shot:06|The Trending strip, with three cards about a finished match. The timestamps are blacked out.}}

What you will see

Everything on your home page comes from what you have done. A new account shows zeroes in every tile and "No rankings yet" under Team rank. The numbers fill in as you play.

Trending is not your feed. It is activity from across Scoryboard, so most of it is about people and teams you have never met.

If it does not work

  • Your statistics are all zero after a match. They are written a short time after the match finishes, not the instant it ends. Reload the page in a minute.
  • A banner about a competition sits across the top. That is a Scoryboard promotion. Select the cross on the right to close it.
  • The sidebar is not there. Some screens, such as the sign-up steps, are drawn without it. Select the Scoryboard logo to get back.
