When somebody adds you to their team, Scoryboard puts the invitation in front of you the next time you sign in.

Accept an invitation

  1. Sign in. If somebody has invited you, you land on Join a team. {{shot:01|The Join a team screen: "Select a team you're invited to join", with one team card showing the crest, the team name and "Mo invited you - 9 Members".}}
  2. Select the team's card. A tick appears on it.
  3. Select Continue. {{shot:02|The Join a team screen with the card selected and the Continue button outlined.}}
  4. Open Teams. The team is in your list, with your role in it. {{shot:03|The Manage Teams screen with the invited team's row outlined, showing the Player badge.}}
  5. Select the team to open its page: its record, its members, its matches. {{shot:04|A team page seen by a member: crest, banner, member and follower counts, and the Team info tab.}}

What you will see

The team is in your Teams list before you accept. You are put on the team sheet the moment the owner adds your address. Accepting is how you confirm it, and it is what changes the icon beside your name on the owner's member list.

If nobody has invited you, the same screen says so.

{{shot:05|The Join a team screen reading "You don't have any pending team invites." with an Open Scoryboard button.}}

If it does not work

  • You were not taken to Join a team. Go to it yourself, or ask whoever invited you to send you the team's invitation link.
  • The screen says you have no invitations, but you were expecting one. Check which address the invitation went to. It has to be the address on your account.
  • You opened an invitation link and were refused. You may have been removed from that team and blocked. Ask the team's Owner.
  • You have no Scoryboard account yet. Create one with the address the invitation was sent to. The invitation is waiting when you finish.
