The padel standings table is on the Results tab. It has six columns, and none of them counts goals.

Finding the table

  1. Open the tournament and select Results. {{shot:01|The Results tab for a padel tournament, showing the Group A standings for four pairs.}}
  2. Select Group Phase. Once every group match has a score, the tab opens on the knockout phase instead.
  3. Read the table. One row per pair. {{shot:02|The padel standings table with its column heading row outlined: PLD, SCORE, W, D, L and PTS.}}
  4. Open Fixtures under the table. Padel matches are grouped by round. {{shot:03|The Fixtures list under the padel standings, grouped under Round 1 and Round 2 headings.}}

What the columns mean

  • PLD - matches played.
  • SCORE - the points the pair won across those matches.
  • W, D, L - won, drawn, lost.
  • PTS - standings points. This is what the table is sorted on.

SCORE and PTS are two different things. SCORE is how much the pair scored on court. PTS is what a result is worth in the table, and you set that yourself: Win points, Loss points and Draw points in Configuration.

What you will see

Matches in one round share a kick-off time and each carries a court number. That is why the fixture list is grouped: a round is everything played at once, across the courts you have.

If it does not work

  • Every number is zero. No match has a score yet.
  • Two pairs have the same PTS but a different order. SCORE separates them.
  • PTS does not match what you expected. Check Win points, Loss points and Draw points in Configuration. A draw is worth 2 by default, not 1.
  • You expected GF, GA and GD. Those are the football columns - see "Reading the group standings table in a football tournament".
