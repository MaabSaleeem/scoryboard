As the referee named on a match, you run it: you start the clock, pause it, record goals and cards, and end the match. You cannot change the match itself - not its date, its venue, its pitch, its note or its line-up.

Before you start

The match's Owner has to have named you as its referee. That is the only thing that gives you these controls. Being a Scoryboard referee on some tournament does not put you on a match, and it makes no difference to what this page lets you do.

What the match page looks like for you

Open the match from your profile or from Schedule. Two things tell you the page has recognised you as its referee.

There are five tabs - MATCH DETAILS, FEED, FACTS, LINEUP and KEYS - and START MATCH beside them. There is no PAYMENT tab and no heading above the tabs.

{{shot:01|The top of a match page. Five tabs read MATCH DETAILS, FEED, FACTS, LINEUP and KEYS. A white "START MATCH" button on the right is outlined in red.}}

Your name is also first in the match's detail row, before the leaderboard, the date and the venue.

{{shot:02|A row of match details. "Rae" beside a referee icon is outlined in red, followed by KB 21 Sunday League, Sat, 5 Dec 2026, KB 21 Astro, 19:00, 5 VS 5 and 60 min.}}

If you see Match Preview (View Only) instead, you are not this match's referee.

Run the match

  1. Select START MATCH at kick-off. There is no confirmation, and a match cannot be un-started.
  2. The button becomes the clock. It counts down from the match duration. {{shot:03|The same five tabs, with a white pill on the right outlined in red. The pill shows a blue pause icon and the time 48:00.}}
  3. Select the clock to pause the match. Select it again to resume. The pause and play icons on the pill tell you which state you are in.
  4. Add goals with + and − under the score. The minus is greyed out at zero. {{shot:04|A rounded row under the score holding two score counters, each reading 0 with a pale grey minus button and a blue plus button. The whole row is outlined in red.}}
  5. Award a card or Player of the Match from the FEED panel. These three appear only once the match is under way. {{shot:05|Three blue outlined buttons in a row, outlined together in red: "Yellow Card" with a yellow square, "Red Card" with a red square, and "Player of Match" with a wreath icon.}}
  6. Pick the team, then the player, and select Save. The card goes into the feed and onto that player's record.
  7. Select END MATCH at full time. The button replaces the clock when the countdown reaches 00:00. {{shot:06|The same five tabs, with a white "END MATCH" button on the right outlined in red.}}

You can also write in the feed while the match is on. Select Comment to add a line of commentary.

What you cannot do

  • Change the match. You get no gear menu, so there is no Edit and no Cancel Match. The date, the venue, the pitch, the duration, the team size and both line-ups are the Owner's.
  • Set the match note. The FEED panel offers you Add Note and it does not work - see below.
  • Cancel or delete the match. Ask the Owner.
  • See or request payments. There is no PAYMENT tab on your version of the page.

If it does not work

  • Add Note saves nothing. The box opens, you type, you select Save, and the note still reads No Note added. Scoryboard refuses the change and says nothing about it. Only the Owner and team Administrators can set a match note. Ask the Owner - see {{link:09.6|Assigning a referee, and adding a banner or note}}.
  • There is no END MATCH. The clock has not reached 00:00. Until it does, the pill is what is there. See {{link:10.4|The match timer}}.
  • You forgot to end the match. Scoryboard ends it for you 24 hours after full time, at whatever the score says then. The details card counts that down as Match auto-ends in. See {{link:10.9|Recording a match that has already been played}}.
  • The match started on its own. A match starts itself when its kick-off time arrives, whether anybody pressed START MATCH or not.
  • The teams read "Add Away Team" or the venue reads "Location not set". Open Teams once and go back to the match. The page needs to have loaded your teams before it draws somebody else's.
  • A match you finished cannot be corrected. A finished match cannot be reopened or rewritten by anybody, including you.
