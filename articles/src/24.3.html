Three screens mean "this is not here", and they look nothing alike. Match yours to the list below, then follow the step.

The three screens

  1. Page not found. The address is wrong. The link you followed points at a page Scoryboard does not have, or you mistyped it. Select Go back to the homepage and use the sidebar instead. {{shot:01|The Scoryboard header and search box above a large white card reading Page not found, with a blue link Go back to the homepage. The footer is below.}}
  2. Team Not Found, or Leaderboard, Tournament or Player Not Found. The address is right and the thing is gone. It was deleted, or the link was copied wrongly. Select Back and search for it by name. {{shot:02|A grey page reading Team Not Found, The team you are looking for does not exist, and a Back to Teams link.}}
  3. This page couldn't load. The page failed while it was loading. Select Reload once. If it comes straight back, read the section below. {{shot:03|A plain white page with a warning triangle, the heading This page couldn't load, the line Reload to try again, or go back, and two buttons, Reload and Back.}}

When "This page couldn't load" comes back

On a leaderboard's settings page, this screen means you are not allowed in. Only the leaderboard's Owner and its Administrators can open settings. Anybody else gets this page every time, and no reload will change that. See {{link:24.1|You do not have permission to do that}}.

Anywhere else, wait a minute and reload again. If it still fails, sign out, sign in, and try once more. Then contact us. See {{link:02.3|Getting help and contacting support}}.

What you will see

Smaller failures do not take over the page. A red line appears under the control you used, such as Something went wrong. Please try again. or Failed to add player. Please try again. Wait a moment and do the same thing again. If it fails three times, the problem is not on your side.

A match address that no longer exists shows the match page empty, with its tabs and nothing in them. There is no Not Found message for a match. Go back to your team page and open the match from there.

If it does not work

  • You keep getting Page not found from the same link. The link is broken. Ask whoever sent it for a new one, or find the page through the sidebar.
  • Reload does nothing. Use your browser's own reload, or close the tab and open Scoryboard again from the homepage.
  • The page loads for a friend and not for you. That is a permission refusal. See {{link:24.1|You do not have permission to do that}}.
