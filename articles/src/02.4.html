Profile settings holds everything about your account in one page. Reach it from your own name in the sidebar.

Open and read Profile settings

  1. Select your name at the top of the sidebar, then select Edit User Profile.
  2. Change your photo or your banner under Profile appearance. Each has its own edit and delete control. {{shot:01|The Profile appearance card, with the banner outlined and an edit and delete control on both the banner and the photo.}}
  3. Change your name, gender, date of birth, sports and preferred position under Personal details. Every field marked with an asterisk is required. Your email address is shown here and cannot be changed. {{shot:02|The Personal details fields: First Name, Last Name, Gender, Date of birth, Sports, Preferred position, Email, and the marketing tick box.}}
  4. Write your bio under My bio, up to 150 characters.
  5. Select Save Changes. Discard throws away anything you have typed since you opened the page. {{shot:03|The Basic information card, with Personal details above My bio, and Save Changes outlined at the foot of it.}}
  6. Read Leaderboards, teams and locations below. It lists every leaderboard and team you belong to, with your role in each, and the venues you have saved. A team you did not create carries a Leave control. {{shot:04|The Leaderboards, Teams and Locations sections, listing two leaderboards, three teams with Owner and Player roles, and an empty Locations panel.}}
  7. Change your password under Security. You need your current one. {{shot:05|The Security section: Current Password, New Password and the Change Password link.}}

What you will see

The page saves nothing until you select Save Changes. Your photo and your banner are the exception: those upload as soon as you apply them.

Deleting your account is at the foot of the same page, under Delete account.

If it does not work

  • Save Changes does nothing. A required field is empty. Scoryboard tints the fields it is waiting for.
  • Your date of birth is refused. You must be at least ten years old to use Scoryboard.
  • Your bio has emptied itself. Changing your profile photo clears it. Write the bio after you have set your photo and banner, then save.
  • There is no Change Password box. Scoryboard offers it only to accounts that sign in with a password. If you sign in another way, a line naming that method takes its place.
