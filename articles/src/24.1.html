Scoryboard decides what you can do by your role on each team, leaderboard and match. Only the Owner and an Administrator can change a team or run its matches. Everybody else can look.

Four screens tell you that you are looking, not running. Find yours below.

Before you start

A refusal that offers a FREE Upgrade (Beta) button is not about your role. It is a Free-plan limit. See {{link:04.3|Every Free-plan limit, and the messages you will see}}.

Which screen are you on?

  1. Access Denied. You opened the settings of a team you do not run. Only the team's Owner and its Administrators can open Edit Team. Nothing you did was saved, and nothing was changed. {{shot:01|A grey page reading Access Denied, You are not allowed to edit this team, and Only team owners and administrators can access team settings.}}
  2. DUMMY TEAM. Somebody created this team as a practice opponent, and only they can open it. There is nothing to see and nobody to join. Select Back to Teams. See {{link:07.2|Dummy teams and unowned teams}}. {{shot:02|A grey page with a padlock drawing, the heading DUMMY TEAM, the line This team is dummy, and you don't have permission to view it, and a Back to Teams link.}}
  3. A greyed-out button on Edit Team. You are an Administrator, and the button is the Owner's. Delete Team is the one: an Administrator sees the section and cannot use it. Everything else on the page is yours to change. {{shot:03|The DELETE TEAM card on Edit Team. A red warning reads This action will delete the team permanently and cannot be undone, and the Delete Team button below it is greyed out and outlined in red.}}
  4. Match Preview (View Only). You opened a match and you are not on the staff of either team. You can read the details, the feed, the facts and the line-ups. You cannot start the match, score it, add a note or request a payment. {{shot:04|The top of a match page. The banner reads Match Preview (View Only) above the tabs MATCH DETAILS, FEED, FACTS, LINEUP and KEYS.}}

What to do

Ask the team's Owner. They open Edit Team, find you under TEAM PLAYERS, and change your role to Administrator. You cannot give yourself a role, and an Owner cannot hand over ownership. See {{link:07.7|Team roles - Owner, Administrator and Player}}.

For a match, the Owner or an Administrator of either team can run it. Ask one of them, or ask them to name you as the match's referee.

If it does not work

  • The page says "This page couldn't load" instead. On a leaderboard's settings, that is a permission refusal wearing a different face. You are not the leaderboard's Owner or an Administrator. See {{link:24.3|Something went wrong, and Page not found}}.
  • You were made an Administrator and the button is still grey. Delete Team stays with the Owner, whatever your role. See {{link:07.11|Deleting a team}}.
  • You are on the team and still get Access Denied. A Player is on the team and cannot open its settings. Only Owner and Administrator can.
  • The match page has no controls but you run one of the teams. Check which team's page you came from. A match between two other teams is view only for you.
