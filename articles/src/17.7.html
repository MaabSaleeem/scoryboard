REQUESTED is the list of everything you have asked for. Open a request to see where each person has got to.

Reading the list

  1. Open Payment. REQUESTED is the tab you land on. {{shot:01|The REQUESTED tab listing four payment requests.}}
  2. The columns tell you what each request is worth and how it is going. {{shot:02|The table headings outlined: Title, Due, Total, Per Person, Paid, Unpaid and Status.}}
  3. Open a request for its amounts and its due date. {{shot:03|The Payment Details window with Per Person, Total and the due date outlined.}}
  4. Below that, each person is listed with their own status. {{shot:04|Two people on the request, each showing a Pending status.}}
  5. A request raised on a team also appears on that team's PAYMENT tab. {{shot:05|The PAYMENT tab on a team page, listing the requests raised against that team.}}

What the columns mean

  • Per Person is the base price - what one player owes.
  • Total is that multiplied by the number of people on the request.
  • Paid and Unpaid count people, not money. "0 / 2" means neither of the two has paid yet.
  • Status is the request as a whole. See {{link:17.9|Payment statuses and failed payments}}.

Both money columns show the base price. The transaction fee is not in this table. To see what a player is actually charged, look at their side of it in {{link:17.8|Paying a request you have received}}.

Where a request appears

Where you raised a request decides where it shows up. One raised against a team appears on that team's PAYMENT tab; one raised against a match appears on the match. Every request you have sent appears under REQUESTED, wherever it came from.
