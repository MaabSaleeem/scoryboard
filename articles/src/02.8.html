Compare puts your statistics next to another player's, row by row. It is a Pro feature.

Before you start

Compare only appears on somebody else's profile. There is no Compare button on your own.

Compare two players

  1. Open another player's profile. Compare is at the right-hand end of the row of counters. {{shot:01|Another player's profile, seen by a Free member, with the Compare button outlined on the right.}}
  2. Select Compare. On Free, Scoryboard offers the upgrade instead. Pro is free during the beta, so FREE Upgrade (Beta) costs nothing and asks for no payment details. {{shot:02|The Unlock Compare with Pro window, explaining that Compare is for Pro members and that Pro (Beta) is free, with a FREE Upgrade (Beta) button.}}
  3. On Pro, the table opens. The left column is you, the right column is the player whose profile you opened. {{shot:03|The Compare players table: ten rows of statistics with two player columns side by side.}}
  4. Read the arrow beside each number. It marks the larger of the two, green for the larger and red for the smaller. Two equal numbers are both green. {{shot:04|The same table with the Win row outlined: 0 with a red arrow in one column, 1 with a green arrow in the other.}}
  5. Select Close when you are done.

What you will see

The ten rows are the same statistics as the tiles on a profile: matches, wins, losses, draws, W/L ratio, Player of the match, goals, assists and the two card colours.

The arrow is arithmetic, not judgement. A player with more losses than you gets a green arrow on the Loss row, because 1 is larger than 0.

If it does not work

  • There is no Compare button. You are looking at your own profile. Open somebody else's.
  • "Unlock Compare with Pro" opens instead of the table. Your account is on Free. Upgrading is free during the beta.
  • Both columns are all zeroes. Neither of you has finished a match yet. Compare reads the same statistics as your profile.
  • You want to compare three players. A player's profile compares two. Close the window and open another profile for the next pair.
