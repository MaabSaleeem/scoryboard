Everything a leaderboard shows comes from matches played inside it. Three tabs hold it: Team Stats is the league table, Player Stats is every player in the league, and Matches is the fixtures.

Before you start

A match only counts towards a leaderboard if it was attached to one. A match played outside this leaderboard adds nothing to any of these three tabs - including a tournament fixture, which carries no leaderboard at all. It still counts on the team's own page; it is simply not in this league.

Read the board

  1. Open Leaderboards and select Open Board. The header names the league, counts the teams in it and counts the views. Team Stats is the tab it opens on. {{shot:01|A leaderboard board: the banner with the crest and Create Match, the Teams and Views tiles below it, the five tabs, and the league table under Team Stats.}}
  2. Read the table. Six columns: Rank, Teams, Members, Matches, Goals and Win/Loss. The top three ranks get a trophy. {{shot:02|The league table: KB 08 United 1st with 2 matches, 5 goals and 2/0; KB 08 Rovers 2nd with 3, 4 and 1/1; KB 08 City 3rd with 3, 2 and 0/2.}}
  3. Open Player Stats for the players. Every player in every team in the league is ranked, with goals, assists and both card counts. {{shot:03|The Player Stats grid, fifteen players ranked. Nia KB is first with 3 goals. The columns are Rank, Players, Teams, Position, Matches, Goals, Assists and two Cards columns.}}
  4. Open Matches. It starts on Upcoming Matches. Each card carries the date, kick-off, team size, venue and the leaderboard it belongs to. {{shot:04|An upcoming fixture card: 17 Sept 2026, 19:00, 5 VS 5, KB 08 Astro, KB 08 Premier League, KB 08 United HOME versus KB 08 City AWAY, tagged League, with a Match Settings button.}}
  5. Select Past Matches for results. The newest match is at the top. Match Results opens the full match. {{shot:05|The Past Matches list: four results, newest first, each with its score and a Match Results button.}}
  6. Use Select teams to narrow the list to one team's fixtures. It offers only the teams in this leaderboard. {{shot:06|The Select teams filter open, listing KB 08 Rovers, KB 08 United and KB 08 City.}}

What you will see

No points column, and no draws. Win/Loss reads "1/1". A draw is the difference between Matches and those two numbers added together: KB 08 Rovers played 3, won 1 and lost 1, so the third was a draw. Nothing on the table says so.

Goals means goals scored. There is no goals-conceded column and no goal difference.

Rank is worked out for you. You cannot sort the table or set the order by hand.

Position is a dash for most players. A player added by name only has no profile, so there is nothing to show. A player with a Scoryboard account has their preferred position.

Teams counts how many teams in this league a player belongs to. Usually 1.

Compare needs Pro. The control above the player grid puts two players side by side. On Free it is locked.

If it does not work

  • The table is empty and reads "No teams data available". No teams have joined yet. See "Adding and removing teams".
  • A team is in the league but has no numbers. None of its matches were attached to this leaderboard. Attaching one afterwards is not possible once the match has been played.
  • A match you played is not in the list. It belongs to a different leaderboard, or to none.
  • The figures did not change after a match finished. Reload the page. Statistics are written a few seconds after the final whistle, and the page does not refresh itself.
  • An upcoming match reads Finish Setup instead of a score. The match has no venue yet, so it is not fully scheduled.
  • You see no Create Match and no Payment tab. You are neither the Owner nor an Administrator. You can still read the table, the player grid and the fixtures.
