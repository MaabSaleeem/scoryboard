Yellow Card and Red Card sit under the match feed, beside Player of Match and Comment.

Before you start

Only the team's Owner and an Administrator can issue a card - see "Starting, pausing and ending a match" for what everybody else sees. The match has to be running.

Book a player

  1. Go to FEED and select Yellow Card. {{shot:01|The four buttons under the feed - Yellow Card, Red Card, Player of Match, Comment - with Yellow Card outlined.}}
  2. Choose the team. Select team is marked with an asterisk because you have to do it first - the player field stays greyed out until you have. {{shot:02|The Yellow Card window: Select team * with both crests, a greyed-out Player field reading Select Player, and a greyed-out Save button.}}
  3. Open Player and choose who to book. Either team's players are offered, so you can book an opponent. {{shot:03|The player list open in the Yellow Card window, showing the away team's players.}}
  4. Select Save. The card joins the feed with a yellow marker. {{shot:04|The feed entry reading "Mo issued Yellow Card to Dara KB", outlined, with a small yellow card beside it.}}
  5. A sending off works the same way. Select Red Card instead. {{shot:05|The Red Card window, the same shape as the yellow one: Select team, then Player.}}
  6. The red card joins the feed with a red marker. {{shot:06|The feed with both cards on it, the red card entry outlined, so the two markers can be told apart.}}

How the final score is set

There is no final-score box to fill in. The score is the two counters, and whatever they read when the match ends is the result.

{{shot:07|A finished match: the score, a trophy on the winning team, a WIN badge, and the notice "The match has ended and cannot be edited".}}

What you will see

Cards do not touch the score. A sending off changes nothing except the feed and the card counts on the FACTS panel.

A drawn match is allowed. A match that ends level ends as a DRAW. Nothing asks you to separate the teams.

There are no penalties here. Penalty shoot-outs belong to a tournament knockout, where a drawn tie has to produce a winner to fill the next round. An ordinary match, league or friendly, never asks.

The score locks at full time. Once the match is ended the counters are gone. Get the score right before you press END MATCH.

If it does not work

  • Select Player will not open. Choose the team first. That field is greyed out until you do.
  • The player you want is not listed. They are not in that team's lineup for this match.
  • You booked the wrong player. Ask whoever runs the team - a card can be corrected from the feed entry while the match is still running.
  • The buttons are not there. The match has not started, or has finished, or you are not the Owner or an Administrator.
  • You are looking for a penalty shoot-out. Set the tie up as a tournament knockout instead.
