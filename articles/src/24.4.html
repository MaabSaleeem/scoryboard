Scoryboard replaces swear words with asterisks wherever people can read them. The Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy are linked at the foot of every page. Everything we hold about you is in Profile settings, and you can delete all of it yourself.

Language filtering

The filter runs when you save, not while you type. A swear word in a comment, a chat message, a team name, a bio or a friend's name is stored with asterisks in place of its letters, and that is what everybody sees, you included. There is no warning and nothing is refused.

  1. Type your comment and select Comment. The word appears as asterisks, one for each letter. The rest of the sentence is untouched. {{shot:01|The Comments panel on a team page. The comment box is empty. Below it, Comments (1) and one comment by Marc reading What a **** result, we were much better than that, outlined in red. The time stamp is blanked out.}}
  2. A posted comment cannot be edited or deleted, so write a new one if you need to. A chat message can be edited or deleted. See {{link:18.3|Replying, forwarding, reacting, editing and deleting}}.

The filter does not catch everything, and it works on English words. If somebody is abusive in chat, report the message. See {{link:18.4|Leaving, deleting and reporting}}.

Terms and privacy

You accepted the Terms and the Privacy Policy when you created your account. Both are linked at the foot of every page, signed in or out.

  1. Scroll to the foot of any page.
  2. Select Privacy Policy or Terms of Service. Each opens on scoryboard.com. {{shot:02|The footer of a page: © 2026 Scoryboard, then Privacy Policy and Terms of Service outlined in red, with social icons to the right.}}

What we store

Your personal details are the ones you gave us at sign-up, and you can change all but one of them in Profile settings.

  1. Open Profile settings from your name in the sidebar and read Personal Details: first name, last name, gender, date of birth, sports, preferred position and email. The tick box below them is your consent to marketing email. {{shot:03|The Personal Details section of Profile settings: First Name Marc, Last Name KB, Gender Male, Date of birth 17-04-1990, Sports Football, Preferred position Striker, a greyed-out Email field, and a ticked box reading Yes! I want early access to new tools, features and limited-time deals!}}
  2. Change what you need to and select Save Changes. The email address is greyed out. It is the address the account was created with, and only support can change it. See {{link:20.3|Why you are not receiving our emails}}.

Beyond these, Scoryboard keeps what you do in the app: your photo and banner, your bio, the teams you are on, your matches and the statistics they produce, your comments and ratings, your chat messages, who you follow, and how many people viewed your profile.

Deleting your account removes it at once. Rows you left on other people's teams keep your name but lose your email address. See {{link:01.7|Deleting your account, and why an account may be disabled}}.

If it does not work

  • A word was masked and it is not a swear word. The filter matches letters, not meaning. Reword the sentence.
  • A swear word got through. The list is not complete. Report the message if it is abusive.
  • You want a copy of your data or a change to your email. Write to us with the address on your account. See {{link:02.3|Getting help and contacting support}}.
