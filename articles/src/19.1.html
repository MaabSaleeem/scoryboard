Comments live in two places: a team page and a leaderboard. Both work the same way.

Before you start

You have to be a member. On a team that means you are on it. On a leaderboard it means you are its Owner, an Administrator, or a player on one of its teams.

Everybody else can read the comments. They cannot add one, reply, or like.

Write a comment

  1. Open your team and scroll down to Comments. The box sits above the existing comments. {{shot:01|The Comments area of a team page. An empty "Write your comment..." box with Add Media and Comment sits above Comments (3) and three comments.}}
  2. Type your comment in the box. {{shot:02|The comment box with "Can somebody bring the corner flags on Tuesday?" typed in it and the blue Comment button outlined.}}
  3. To attach a photo, select Add Media and choose an image. It appears as a thumbnail under your text. {{shot:03|The same box with the typed comment and a small thumbnail of a shirt below it, outlined, with a cross in its corner for removing it.}}
  4. Select Comment. Your comment goes to the top of the list. {{shot:04|A posted comment reading "New kit arrived this morning." with its attached picture of a shirt below it, outlined.}}

On a leaderboard

The panel is identical. Open the leaderboard and scroll down, or select the Comments tab, which scrolls you to the same place.

{{shot:05|The Comments area of a leaderboard, with the same box, the same Add Media and Comment buttons, and two comments below.}}

{{link:08.5|Sharing, commenting on and deleting a leaderboard}} covers this from the leaderboard's side.

What you will see

The heading counts comments, not replies. Comments (3) means three comments. Their replies are folded up behind View n more replies.

Photos only. The attachment picker takes images. It does not take video.

You cannot take a comment back. There is no edit and no delete, on your own comments or anybody else's. Read it before you send it.

If the box is greyed out

You are not a member. The whole panel still shows, and every control in it is switched off - the box, Add Media, Comment and every Reply. Nothing tells you why.

{{shot:06|The same Comments area seen by somebody who is not on the team. The box, Add Media, Comment and the Reply links are all greyed out. The box is outlined.}}

Ask the team's Owner to add you, or the leaderboard's Owner to make you an Administrator.

If it does not work

  • You cannot find a comment box on a match. There is not one. A match has a feed instead - see {{link:10.8|The match feed and commentary}}.
  • You cannot find one on a player or a tournament either. Teams and leaderboards are the only two places comments appear.
  • The Comment button will not light up. The box is empty. Type something, or attach a photo.
  • You posted the wrong thing. It cannot be edited or removed. Add a second comment correcting it.
