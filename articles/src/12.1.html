A tournament goes live the moment you create it. What changes as you set it up is how much of it there is to show.

Before you start

You need a Scoryboard account. Anyone can create a tournament. Only the owner and the admins can change one.

The two states you will see most

Open Tournament in the sidebar to see every tournament you own or help run.

{{shot:01|The Tournament list showing two tournaments. KB Cup has a Round Robin label under its name; KB New Cup has none.}}

A tournament with a format shows that format under its name, here Round Robin. A tournament without one shows only the game type. That label is the quickest way to tell a tournament you have finished setting up from one you have not.

Open a tournament that has no format and its Format tab still asks you to pick one.

{{shot:02|The Format tab on a tournament with no format, showing the three template cards: Group phase only, Knockout phase only, and Group and knockout phase.}}

Nothing is scheduled yet. Choosing a format is what creates the groups and generates the fixtures.

What everyone else sees

Every tournament has a public page. It shows the date, the kick-off time, the teams, the groups, the venues and the format.

{{shot:03|The public tournament page on the Info tab, showing tournament date, start time, total teams, number of groups, total matches, number of locations, the venue and the tournament format.}}

Your setup screens are not on this page. Visitors get the read-only tabs: Info, Participants, Standings, Matches, Leaderboard and Chat.

What you will see

A tournament is published from the moment you create it. It becomes live when its first match starts, and completed when you set a winner. You do not publish it yourself and there is no draft to come back to, so give it a name you are happy for people to see.

If it does not work

  • The list says you have no tournaments. It shows an empty count for a moment while it loads. Give it a second before you create a second one by mistake.
  • You cannot find a tournament you were added to. The list shows tournaments you own or administer. If someone added your team, look under Teams instead.
  • Your format is missing from the card. The format only appears once you have saved it on the Format tab. Opening the tab is not enough.
