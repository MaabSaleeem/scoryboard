A leaderboard needs teams before it can show anything. Add them from the Teams section of its settings screen.

Before you start

Only the Owner and an Administrator can add or remove teams.

Removing a team happens on the click. There is no confirmation and no undo button. Read the last step before you use it.

Add a team

  1. Open the leaderboard's settings screen and find Teams. The teams already in the league are listed under Teams joined. {{shot:01|The Teams section of Edit Leaderboard with three teams listed, and the Add Team button outlined at the top right.}}
  2. Select Add Team. Continue stays greyed out until you choose a team. {{shot:02|The Add Team window with the outlined "Search for a team" box and a greyed-out Continue button.}}
  3. Select the box and type the team's name. Teams already in the league are left out of the results. {{shot:03|The Add Team window with "KB 08" typed. One result, KB 08 Athletic, is outlined below the box.}}
  4. Choose the team from the list. Its name fills the box and Continue turns blue. {{shot:04|The Add Team window with KB 08 Athletic in the box and the Continue button outlined.}}
  5. Select Continue. The team appears at the bottom of Teams joined. {{shot:05|The Teams section now listing four teams. The new row, KB 08 Athletic, is outlined.}}
  6. To take a team out, select Remove beside it. The team goes straight away - nothing asks you to confirm. {{shot:06|The Teams section with four teams. The Remove control beside KB 08 Athletic is outlined.}}

What you will see

The table changes immediately. A team you add brings its matches in this leaderboard with it, so the league table and the player figures both change. A team you remove takes them out again.

You can add a team you do not own. The search covers every team on Scoryboard, not just yours. Type enough of the name to be sure you have the right one.

Only your own teams show before you type. Opening the box offers the teams you own that are not in the league yet. Typing widens the search.

If it does not work

  • The team you want is not in the results. It is already in the league. Check Teams joined above.
  • Continue will not turn on. You typed a name but did not choose it from the list. Select the row.
  • You removed the wrong team. Add it back with Add Team. Its matches and figures come back with it.
  • The table did not change after you added a team. The team has no matches tagged into this leaderboard. A match only counts towards a leaderboard if it was attached to it.
  • You cannot open the settings screen. You are not the Owner and not an Administrator. See "Your leaderboard settings screen".
