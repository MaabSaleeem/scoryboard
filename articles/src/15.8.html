Results is where you run the tournament. Each fixture is a card, and each card has three states.

Before you start

You need to be the Owner or an Administrator of the tournament. A referee can do this too, if you gave them Allow referee to start and end matches when you added them.

Entering a result

  1. Open your tournament and choose Results. A fixture that has not started reads Scheduled and carries a START button. {{shot:01|A Scheduled fixture card with the START button outlined, the teams separated by VS, and no score boxes.}}
  2. Choose START. The card goes Live. START becomes END, and two score boxes appear between the teams. {{shot:02|The same card, now Live, with an END button and two empty score boxes, the home box outlined.}}
  3. Type the score. It saves as you type: there is no Save button and no confirmation. {{shot:03|The Live card with 3 in the home box and 1 in the away box.}}
  4. Choose END when the match is over. A trophy marks the winner. {{shot:04|The card reading Ended, with a trophy beside the winning team and the score 3 - 1.}}
  5. The table above updates itself. {{shot:05|The Group A standings: played, won, drawn, lost, points, goals for, goals against and goal difference for four teams.}}
  6. The LEADERBOARD tab ranks the teams and names the leader. {{shot:06|The Leaderboard tab, headed Well played KB 15 Falcons, with the top three as cards showing Members, Matches, Goals and Win/Loss.}}

On a padel tournament

  1. The fixtures are grouped under ROUND 1, ROUND 2 and so on, and the standings carry a SCORE column instead of the three goal columns. {{shot:07|A padel Results tab: the standings with PLD, SCORE, W, D, L and PTS, and the fixtures grouped by round.}}
  2. Padel also lists the rules for the format you chose. {{shot:08|A Rules and Regulations panel headed Swiss, listing seven numbered rules.}}
  3. On the Leaderboard the stat column reads Score, where football reads Goals. {{shot:09|A padel Leaderboard, headed Well played Player 1 & Player 2, with a Score column.}}

What you will see

Neither START nor END asks you to confirm, and nor does the score. Everything is saved the moment you do it.

A score can still be corrected after you press END. What closes score entry is ending the phase - after that the boxes are gone and the score is plain text. Read {{link:13.11|Tournament phases - preview, start, end and undo}} to end a phase.

On a narrow screen the eight standings columns do not fit, and a View Stats control opens them in a panel instead.

If it does not work

  • There are no score boxes. The fixture has not been started. Press START.
  • There is no START button either. The phase has been ended. Nothing can be scored after that.
  • The table has not changed. Give it a moment; it refetches after each score.
  • You entered the wrong score. Type over it. The boxes stay editable until the phase ends.
