Sponsors are a list on your tournament. They show on your public page and, if you want, on a screen at the venue.

Before you start

You need to be the Owner or an Administrator of the tournament. Have the sponsor's banner image ready if they have one.

Adding a sponsor

  1. Open your tournament and choose the Sponsor tab, then Add Sponsor. {{shot:01|The Sponsor tab of a tournament with no sponsors, reading No Sponsors Yet, with the Add Sponsor button outlined.}}
  2. Give the sponsor a name. It is the only thing you must fill in. {{shot:02|The Add Sponsor dialog: Sponsor Banner, Sponsor Name, Sponsor Link and a description counted to 180.}}
  3. Add a banner if they have one. Without it the sponsor shows as its initials.
  4. Add their web address if you want the banner to link to it, and a description of up to 180 characters.
  5. Choose Save. The sponsor joins the list. {{shot:03|The Sponsor tab with two sponsors: one showing its banner, one showing its initials.}}

Where sponsors appear

  1. On your public page, in a row above the tabs. Each card links to the address you gave. {{shot:04|Two sponsor cards on the public page: one a green banner, one the letters K R.}}
  2. On a venue screen, if your slide show has a Sponsors slide. "Building a match-day slideshow" covers adding one. {{shot:05|A fullscreen slide showing both sponsors, one as a banner and one as an empty card with its name below.}}
  3. On the Sponsor tab itself, which is your own list of them.

What you will see

A sponsor with no banner still appears. It gets a plain card with its initials, and its name below it on a slide. If you want the logo, add the banner.

If it does not work

  • Save is refused. The name is required. Everything else is optional.
  • Your sponsor shows as two letters. There is no banner on it. Edit the sponsor and add one.
  • The banner has not appeared yet. It is fetched separately from the row, so give it a moment.
  • A Sponsors slide has nothing to offer. Add the sponsor here first, then pick it on the slide.
