A padel tournament does not use the football templates. It has five formats of its own, and a configuration step that asks about courts, rounds and scoring.

Before you start

Create the tournament first and set its Enrollment type - singles or doubles. The format questions are built around that answer.

The five formats

Open the tournament, then Format.

{{shot:01|The padel Choose a format screen with five cards: Swiss, Americano, Mexicano, King of the Court and Round Robin.}}
  • Swiss - fixed pairs play rounds against teams with similar results.
  • Americano - a social format where everyone gets to play with everyone.
  • Mexicano - a balanced competition that pairs players of similar skill levels.
  • King of the Court - a self-sorting competition, where pairs fight to claim the throne.
  • Round Robin - classic league-style, where everyone plays in a set rotation.

Setting up Swiss

  1. Select Swiss. The configuration appears underneath. {{shot:02|The Swiss card selected and outlined, with the configuration fields starting below it.}}
  2. Choose a Standings type. Individual ranks each player on their own results. Team ranks the pair together. {{shot:03|The configuration with the Standings type control outlined, offering Individual and Team.}}
  3. Choose Scoring - the points a match is played to. Pick 12, 24 or 32, or set your own with Custom. {{shot:04|The configuration with the Scoring field outlined, set to 24.}}
  4. Choose How many players would be there? The list goes up in fours, from 4 to 32. {{shot:05|The player count list open, showing 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 and 28 players.}}
  5. Set How many rounds do you want to create?, the number of Courts you have, and the Gap between rounds in minutes. {{shot:06|The completed Swiss configuration with the rounds field outlined, alongside courts, scoring and the gap between rounds.}}

    The panel underneath spells out how the format you picked actually works. It is worth reading before you commit.

    {{shot:07|The Rules and regulations panel for Swiss, listing seven rules.}}
  6. Select Next.

What you will see

Scoryboard builds the tournament: a placeholder pair for every two players, a group phase holding them, and a knockout phase with the Swiss finals bracket.

{{shot:08|The saved tournament showing a Swiss badge, four pairs in Group A, and a knockout phase with semi-finals and a final.}}

The pairs are named Player 1 & Player 2 and so on. Rename them from the Participants tab as your entries come in.

If it does not work

  • Next does nothing. The player count is required, and the message underneath reads "Enter a valid number of teams." Pick a number from the list.
  • You cannot type any number of players. The list only offers multiples of four, because padel is played two against two.
  • You expected the football templates. Group phase, knockout phase and group & knockout are the Football and Other Sports set. Padel has its own five.
  • Too many rounds for the courts you have. Rounds, courts and the gap between them decide how long the day runs. Check the Schedule tab afterwards.
