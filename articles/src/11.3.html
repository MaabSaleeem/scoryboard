Your statistics update within a few seconds of a match ending. The page does not refresh itself, so reload it to see the new figures.

One thing has to be true first: the match has to be finished. A match that is still Live, Paused or Incomplete writes nothing, however long you wait.

Two kinds of finished match count. A match in a leaderboard, and a tournament fixture. There is no third kind: an ordinary match with no leaderboard never leaves Incomplete, so it can never be played.

Check the match, then reload

  1. Open the match. The MATCH DETAILS card has to read MATCH ENDED. On a leaderboard match, the strip along the bottom names the leaderboard. {{shot:01|A finished match: the MATCH ENDED pill, the score, and the strip below naming KB 11 Sunday League, which is outlined.}}
  2. Reload Home. Your tiles carry the new figures. The LEADERBOARDS table at the foot of the page shows which leaderboard they came from. {{shot:02|The Leaderboards table on Home, with the KB 11 Sunday League row outlined: rank, teams, matches, goals, assists and two card columns.}}

What you will see

Seconds, not minutes. The figures are ready within ten seconds of the final whistle. You get no notification, so reload the page to see them. If a minute has passed and nothing has changed, the cause is below.

The same figures appear in three places. Your tiles on Home, your team's tiles, and the team's PLAYER STATS table.

The LEADERBOARDS table is the fourth place, and it counts less. Each row is one leaderboard and holds only that leaderboard's own matches. A tournament fixture is in your tiles and in none of those rows.

A leaderboard with no matches reads zero. Your account comes with a leaderboard of its own. It stays at zero until a match is played in it.

If it does not work

  • No leaderboard is named under the score. Check whether it is a tournament fixture. A tournament fixture has no leaderboard and still counts towards your tiles. An ordinary match with no leaderboard was never played, and a finished match cannot be moved into a leaderboard afterwards.
  • The match does not read MATCH ENDED. A match that is still Live, Paused or Incomplete writes nothing.
  • Your team's figures moved and yours did not. You were not in the lineup for that match. Your figures count only the matches you were named in. See "How team and player statistics are calculated".
  • The match is in your Matches list but not in your count. The list shows your teams' fixtures. The count shows the matches you were named in.
  • Your tiles moved and the LEADERBOARDS table did not. The match was a tournament fixture. That table counts leaderboard matches only.
  • You reloaded and nothing changed. Check the score on the match. Goals entered after the match ended were not recorded, because a finished match cannot be edited.
