Statistics are written within a few seconds of a match ending. Nothing announces it, and the page you are looking at does not refresh itself - so if your numbers have not moved, reload.

Two things have to be true. The match has to be finished, and it has to belong to a leaderboard. A match outside a leaderboard is never counted, however long you wait.

Check the match, then reload

  1. Open the match. The MATCH DETAILS card has to read MATCH ENDED, and the strip along the bottom has to name a leaderboard. {{shot:01|A finished match: the MATCH ENDED pill, the score, and the strip below naming KB 11 Sunday League, which is outlined.}}
  2. Reload Home. Your statistic tiles carry the new figures, and the LEADERBOARDS table at the foot of the page shows which leaderboard they came from. {{shot:02|The Leaderboards table on Home, with the KB 11 Sunday League row outlined: rank, teams, matches, goals, assists and two card columns.}}

What you will see

A few seconds, not minutes. On the matches used to write this article the figures were ready within seven seconds of the final whistle. If a minute has passed and nothing has changed, the cause is one of the ones below, not the wait.

Nothing tells you it has happened. There is no notification and no badge. Reload the page you are on.

Your figures land in more than one place. Your own tiles on Home, the LEADERBOARDS table under them, your team's tiles, and the team's PLAYER STATS table all read the same numbers.

A leaderboard with no matches reads zero across. Your account is given a leaderboard of its own when you sign up, and it sits in that table with nothing in it until a match is played in it. That is normal.

If it does not work

  • The match is not in a leaderboard. Nothing will ever be written for it. Check the strip under the score: if no leaderboard is named there, the match is not counted. A finished match cannot be moved into one.
  • The match is not finished. A match that is still Live, Paused or Incomplete writes nothing. It has to read MATCH ENDED.
  • You were not in the lineup. Your figures count matches you were named in. Your team's figures count all of them, so the team's numbers can move while yours do not. See "How team and player statistics are calculated".
  • The match is in your Matches list but not in your count. Same cause. The list shows your teams' fixtures; the count shows the ones you played.
  • You reloaded and nothing changed. Check the score on the match itself. If the goals were entered after the match ended, they were not recorded - a finished match cannot be edited.
