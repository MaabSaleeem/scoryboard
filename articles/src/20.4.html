Trending is a strip of cards near the bottom of your home page. It is a running record of what is happening on Scoryboard - teams being created, matches kicking off, players joining and winning.

Read the strip

  1. Open the home page and scroll down to TRENDING. {{shot:01|The Trending panel on the home page. Under the heading TRENDING, three cards side by side: "KB Notify FC just won their game", "Match summary: KB Notify FC 1 - 0 KB Notify Rovers" and "Match between KB Notify FC and KB Notify Rovers has finished". Each card carries a team crest, an emoji, the blue label MATCH and how long ago it happened.}}
  2. Read a card. Under the sentence is a blue word and a time. The word says what kind of thing the card is about: MATCH, TEAM or PLAYER. The time says how long ago it happened. {{shot:02|One Trending card, showing a crest, a trophy emoji, and "KB Notify FC just won their game". The row beneath it is outlined in red: the blue word MATCH, a dot, and how long ago it happened.}}
  3. Use the arrows under the strip to move along it. The left arrow is greyed out while you are on the first card. {{shot:03|The Trending panel with its three cards. Under them, two small round arrow buttons are outlined in red together. The left arrow is grey and the right arrow is dark.}}
  4. Select a card to open whatever it is about - the match, the team or the player. The names inside the card are separate links: selecting one opens that team or player instead. {{shot:04|A Trending card reading "Match summary: KB Notify FC 1 - 0 KB Notify Rovers". The sentence is outlined in red, and both team names inside it are in bold blue.}}

What you will see

Trending is not your own feed. It is activity from across Scoryboard, not only from your teams and the people you follow. You will see teams and players you have nothing to do with.

It moves on its own. The strip scrolls to the next card every few seconds. Use the arrows to go back.

It is newest first, and it goes on: more cards load as you move along the strip.

Your own news is in your notifications and your email, not here. See {{link:20.1|Your notifications - reading, marking and deleting}}.

If it does not work

  • You cannot find Trending. It is below your statistics on the home page. Scroll down.
  • A card is about people you do not know. That is normal. Trending covers the whole of Scoryboard.
  • Your own match is not in it. Trending shows a match when it is created, started, finished and won. A match that has done none of those yet is not there. Your own matches are on your home page under MATCHES.
  • The left arrow does nothing. You are on the first card. It turns dark once you have moved along.
  • You want to see only your own teams. There is no filter on Trending. Use your notifications instead.
