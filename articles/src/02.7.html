Four panels on your home page answer "what have I actually played": Teams, Team rank, Matches and the leaderboards table.

Read your record

  1. Open your home page.
  2. Read Teams. It lists every team you are in, whether you own it or joined it. Every account starts with two teams named after it. {{shot:01|The Teams panel listing three teams.}}
  3. Read Team rank. It says where each of your teams sits in the leaderboard it plays in. {{shot:02|The Team rank panel reading "1st Place in KB 02 Rovers".}}
  4. Select Past Matches in the Matches panel. It opens on Upcoming Matches, so you have to switch it. {{shot:03|The Matches panel with the Past Matches tab outlined, showing a finished match: KB 02 Rovers 2 - 1 KB 02 City, League, 20 Aug 2026, 19:00, 5 VS 5, Otto's leaderboard.}}
  5. Read the match card. It carries the score, the tag, which side was at home, the date, the kick-off time, the team size and the leaderboard the match counted towards.
  6. Scroll to the Leaderboards table at the foot of the page. Each row is a leaderboard you are in, with your rank and your totals in it. {{shot:04|The Leaderboards table with two rows, showing rank, teams, matches, goals, assists and cards.}}

What you will see

Team rank stays on "No rankings yet" until one of your teams has finished a match in a leaderboard.

A leaderboard you have joined but not yet played in shows a dash instead of a rank.

If it does not work

  • A match you played is not in the list. Check Upcoming Matches as well. A match only moves to Past Matches once it has been finished.
  • Your team is in the list but has no rank. The team is not in a leaderboard, or it has not played a leaderboard match yet.
  • Your totals look too low straight after a match. They are written a short time after the match ends. Reload the page in a minute.
