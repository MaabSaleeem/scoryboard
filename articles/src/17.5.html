Taking a payment costs money. Scoryboard lets you decide who carries that cost, and you decide it per request.

Before you start

The choice sits in the Payment Request form, under the base price. It is set for each request and cannot be changed once the request has gone out.

Choosing who pays the fee

  1. Set your Base Price. The fee appears underneath it.
  2. Add transaction fee to price is on by default. Your players pay the total, and you receive the base price in full. {{shot:01|The fee breakdown with the toggle on, reading: You will receive £10.00, Transaction fee £0.56, Total price £10.56.}}
  3. Turn it off to carry the fee yourself. Your players pay the base price, and the fee comes out of your payout. {{shot:02|The same block with the toggle off and no breakdown shown.}}
  4. The line underneath describes the switched-on behaviour, and when your payout arrives. {{shot:03|The helper text under the toggle, reading that recipients pay the total price and will not see the transaction fee.}}

What your players are charged

  1. With the fee passed on, a £10 request costs each player £10.56. {{shot:04|A player's PAY list with a £10 pass-on request outlined, showing £10.56.}}
  2. With the fee absorbed, a £15 request costs each player exactly £15.00, and you receive less than £15. {{shot:05|The same PAY list with a £15 absorbed request outlined, showing £15.00.}}

What you will see

The fee is worked out from your base price. At £10 it is 56p, giving a total of £10.56.

Your payout arrives up to 7 days after a request has been paid.

Turning the fee off hides the breakdown, so the form stops telling you what you will receive. To see the fee for your amount, turn the toggle back on and read it off before you decide.
