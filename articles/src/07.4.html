Everything you can change about a team is on one screen, Edit Team. The Owner and any Administrator can open it. A Player or a Fan cannot.

Open Edit Team and read it

  1. Open Teams. Select the menu at the end of the team's row and select Edit. {{shot:01|A team row's menu open on the Manage Teams screen, offering Edit and Remove, with Edit outlined.}}
  2. Read the page. It is five cards, top to bottom. {{shot:02|The whole Edit Team page: Profile appearance, Team information, Team players, Leaderboards and Delete team.}}
  3. PROFILE APPEARANCE holds the banner and the crest, each with its own edit and delete control. {{shot:03|The Profile appearance card with the banner, the crest and their controls.}}
  4. TEAM INFORMATION holds the name, the team size and the bio. Nothing here is saved until you select Save Changes. {{shot:04|The Team information card: Team name, Team size, Bio, and the Discard and Save Changes buttons.}}
  5. TEAM PLAYERS lists everybody on the team with their role and their position, and is where you add somebody new. {{shot:05|The Team players card listing nine members with Owner, Admin and Player badges and their positions.}}
  6. LEADERBOARDS shows the leaderboards the team has joined, and lets you add or remove one. {{shot:06|The Leaderboards card with an Add Leaderboard button and one joined leaderboard with a Remove link.}}
  7. DELETE TEAM is last, and only the Owner can use it. An Administrator sees the section with the button greyed out. {{shot:07|The Delete team card seen by an Administrator, with the disabled Delete Team button outlined.}}

What you will see

Your own row in TEAM PLAYERS is marked (You) and has no menu. You cannot change your own role or take yourself off the team from here.

A member added by name rather than by email has no position and carries an Invite button instead.

If it does not work

  • The team's row has no Edit. You are a Player or a Fan on that team. Only the Owner and Administrators can edit it.
  • Delete Team is greyed out. You are an Administrator, not the Owner. Only the Owner can delete a team.
  • Your changes did not stick. The name, size and bio need Save Changes. The images do not - they save as soon as you apply the crop.
