Reset your password from the sign-in screen. Scoryboard emails you a link, and the link takes you to a form for the new one.

Reset your password

  1. On the sign-in screen, select Forgot password? {{shot:01|The sign-in form with the Forgot password? link outlined.}}
  2. Enter the email address you sign in with. {{shot:02|The Forgot password? screen with an address entered and the Send reset link button outlined.}}
  3. Select Send reset link. Scoryboard confirms where it sent the email. {{shot:03|The Check Your Email screen, naming the address the reset link went to.}}
  4. Open the email and select the reset link. It opens Reset your password.
  5. Enter your new password twice, then select Reset Password. {{shot:04|The Reset your password form with both fields filled in and the Reset Password button outlined.}}

What you will see

Resetting signs you in straight away. You land on your home page, already signed in with the new password.

Setting a password on an invited account

A team owner can add you to a team by email before you have an account. You get an email headed You're Invited.

You have no password yet, because you have no account yet. Forgot password? will not help you. It reports that it has sent a link, and nothing arrives.

Do this instead. Open the invitation and select the link in it. It opens the sign-in screen with your address already filled in. Select Create an account, and sign up with that same address. Set your password there.

Once you have finished signing up, the team invitation is waiting for you at Set up your team. Select Join to accept it.

If it does not work

  • No email arrives. Check your spam folder. If there is still nothing, you probably have no account with that address - see the section above.
  • "An unknown error occurred during password reset." The link has already been used, or the new password is the one you had before. Ask for a fresh link and choose a different password.
  • You signed up with Google or Apple. Your account has no password to reset. Sign in with the button you signed up with.
