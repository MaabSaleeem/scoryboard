Your tournament already has a public page. There is nothing to publish and no switch to turn on.

Before you start

You see the organiser board if you are the Owner or an Administrator of the tournament. Everybody else gets the public page, whether they are signed in or not.

The board and the page

  1. Open Tournament in the sidebar and choose your tournament. This is your board: nine tabs, and nobody else can see them. {{shot:01|The organiser board for KB 15 Cup, with the row of nine tabs outlined.}}
  2. Use Share tournament in the header to get the link to your public page. "Sharing your tournament link and QR code" covers what is in that dialog. {{shot:02|The tournament header with the Share tournament control outlined.}}
  3. Open the link. Anybody can, with or without an account. {{shot:03|The public tournament page, signed out, with Sign In where the sidebar would be.}}
  4. The page has six tabs. {{shot:04|The public tab strip: INFO, PARTICIPANTS, STANDINGS, MATCHES, LEADERBOARD and CHAT.}}

What is on each tab

  1. INFO is the summary. It is built from your setup: the date, the kick-off time, how many teams and groups, how many matches, the venues and the format. Anything you have written on your info page sits above it.
  2. STANDINGS opens on your last phase. A Group and Knockout tournament therefore lands on the bracket. Choose Group Phase for the group table. {{shot:05|The public Standings tab with Group Phase selected, showing Group A with played, won, drawn, lost, points, goals for, goals against and goal difference.}}
  3. MATCHES splits into Past Matches and Upcoming Matches. {{shot:06|The public Matches tab, Upcoming Matches, showing three fixtures with their date, time, venue and teams.}}
  4. PARTICIPANTS lists the teams, and their players and referees. {{shot:07|The public Participants tab, Teams, listing eight teams with how many players each has.}}
  5. LEADERBOARD ranks the teams and names the leader. {{shot:08|The public Leaderboard tab, headed Well played KB 15 Blues, with the top three teams as cards.}}
  6. CHAT is the tournament chat room. A visitor is asked to sign in before they can read it.

What you will see

Before you have set anything up, the page still exists. It reads in zeros.

{{shot:09|The public page of a tournament that has not been configured, showing 0 Teams joined and zeros in every figure.}}

You choose which of the six tabs a visitor sees. "The presentation editor, info page and gallery" covers that.

If it does not work

  • You cannot find a Publish button. There is not one. Your tournament is public from the moment you create it.
  • The standings look empty. The tab opens on your last phase. Choose the phase you want.
  • A tab is missing. Somebody has turned it off in the presentation editor.
  • Match Settings on a fixture is not settings. It opens a read-only preview of that match. It changes nothing, and it is there for everybody.
