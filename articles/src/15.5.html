Your slide show has its own web address. Open it on a screen at the venue and leave it running.

Before you start

Build and save a slide show first. Read {{link:15.4|Building a match-day slideshow}} to do that.

Putting it on a screen

  1. Open your tournament, choose Presentation, then Slideshow, then Open Fullscreen. It opens in a new tab. {{shot:01|The Slide shows card with the Open Fullscreen button outlined.}}
  2. Copy that address to the screen at your venue. Nobody has to sign in. {{shot:02|The fullscreen slide show: the tournament name, a welcome line, and a QR code panel reading Follow this tournament.}}
  3. Leave it. The show turns its own slides, each for the number of seconds you set. {{shot:03|The next slide: Group A's table beside its six finished fixtures.}}

What you will see

The tournament name sits at the top left if you asked for it, and the time at the top right. Powered by Scoryboard is at the foot of every slide.

A QR code slide sends anybody with a phone straight to your public page, so spectators can follow the tournament from their seat.

If it does not work

  • The screen says there are no slides. You have not saved a show yet, or the show is not Active. {{shot:04|A dark screen reading No active slides available.}}
  • A slide is skipped. Its Active tick is off.
  • The slides move too fast or too slowly. Each slide has its own number of seconds. Change it in the editor and save.
  • You do not want the clock. Turn off Show current time on the show's settings.
