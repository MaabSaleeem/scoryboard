A team's page is its record. Open it from Teams by selecting the team's name.

Read your team's page

  1. Read the top of the page. The banner, the crest and the name sit above three counters: Members, Followers and Views. {{shot:01|A team page header: banner, crest, team name, team size, rating, Follow button, and the Members, Followers and Views counters.}}
  2. Stay on Team Info and read the tiles. They are the team's whole record: matches, wins, losses, draws, win-loss ratio, clean sheets, winning streak, goals and conceded, and cards. {{shot:02|Ten statistic tiles reading Matches 1, Win 1, Loss 0, Draw 0, W/L Ratio 1:0, Clean Sheets 0, Winning Streaks 1, Goals / Conceded 3/1, and two card counts at 0.}}
  3. Read TOP SCORER and MOST ASSISTS underneath. {{shot:03|The Top scorer and Most assists panels, each naming one player and a number.}}
  4. Open Matches and select Past Matches. The panel opens on Upcoming, so you have to switch it. {{shot:04|The Matches tab with the Past Matches control outlined, showing a finished match: KB 07 United 3 - 1 KB 07 Rovers, League, 20 Aug 2026, 19:00.}}
  5. Open Player Stats for the same numbers broken down by person, ranked. {{shot:05|The Player Stats table: Rank, Players, Position, Matches, Goals, Assists and two card columns, with five ranked players and four unranked.}}

What you will see

Two kinds of match count. A match played in a leaderboard, and a tournament fixture. A tournament fixture has no leaderboard at all and still adds to every tile on this page, and to the Player Stats table with it.

There is no third kind. An ordinary match with no leaderboard never leaves Incomplete, so it can never be played, so it can never count. See "Match statuses, and why a match is stuck on Incomplete".

A player with no finished matches has a dash for a rank instead of a place.

The positions in the table are the ones each player set on their own profile. A member added by name only has none, and shows a dash.

Followers is a count, not a list. There is no screen that names the people following a team.

If it does not work

  • Every tile reads 0 but you have played matches. Check that the matches finished. A match that is still Live, Paused or Incomplete writes nothing, and an ordinary match cannot be played at all until it is in a leaderboard.
  • Your numbers look wrong just after a match. They are written a short time after the match finishes. Reload the page in a minute.
  • The Matches tab looks empty. It opens on Upcoming Matches. Switch to Past Matches.
  • A player is missing from Player Stats. Everybody on the team sheet is listed. If they are not, they are not on the team.
