Your account starts with one leaderboard already made for you, so the leaderboard limit is reached from your first day.

What you will see

All eight messages are the same window, with different words: a title, the limit in a sentence, and a FREE Upgrade (Beta) button. Selecting that button upgrades you on the tap and costs nothing during the beta.

Two of the limits behave in ways worth knowing about.

  • Adding a friend to a team takes them out of your friends list, and frees a slot against the 14. So the count you see on the Friends screen is the count that matters.
  • Adding a brand-new person to a team also makes them a friend. On a full friends list that is refused with the friend message, not a team one - even though you were adding somebody to a team.

The count of profile views is visible on Free. It is the list of names that is Pro.

If it does not work

  • You are under 14 friends and still refused. Count the Friends screen, not the people on your teams. A friend on one of your teams no longer appears in the list.
  • You were adding somebody to a team and got the friend message. That is expected. Adding a new name to a team creates a friend record first, and your list is full. Remove a friend or upgrade.
  • You want a second leaderboard and do not want Pro. Delete the one you have. The refusal says so.
  • The Views counter does nothing. Nobody has viewed your profile yet, or you are looking at somebody else's. The counter only opens on your own profile, and only once the count is above zero.
  • You upgraded and a gate is still there. Reload the page.
