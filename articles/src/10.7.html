Before you start

Only the team's Owner and an Administrator can choose - see "Starting, pausing and ending a match" for what everybody else sees. The match has to be running.

  1. Go to FEED and select Player of Match. {{shot:01|The four buttons under the feed with Player of Match outlined.}}
  2. Choose the team, then the player. Both teams are offered. {{shot:02|The Player Of Match window: Select team with both crests, then a Player field reading Select Player.}}
  3. Select Save. The choice joins the feed in the middle, with a laurel marker. {{shot:03|The feed entry headed "Player of the match" with the player's name and avatar beneath it, outlined, among the goals and cards.}}

What you will see

You do not have to wait for full time. Choose at any point while the match is running. You can change your choice until it ends.

A laurel marks the player. It is one of the markers the KEYS panel explains.

It counts towards their record. Player of the Match appears in the team's statistics.

If it does not work

  • The button is not there. The match has not started, or it has finished, or you are not the Owner or an Administrator.
  • Save will not work. Choose the team first. The player field stays greyed out until you do.
  • The player you want is not listed. They are not in either lineup for this match.
  • The match has ended. Nothing can be added to a finished match.
