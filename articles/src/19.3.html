Ratings all start in one place: the Rate button on a match that has finished.

Before you start

The match has to be over. Rate only appears once a match is finished. It is at the top right of the match screen.

You cannot rate yourself. Your own name is there but the stars will not respond.

Rate something

  1. Open the finished match and select Rate. {{shot:01|A finished match screen headed Match Result, with the Rate button at the top right outlined.}}
  2. Choose what you are rating. {{shot:02|The "What would you like to rate?" window offering four rows: Match, Player, Team and Referee.}}
  3. Select a star rating from one to five. Add a review if you want to - it is optional and holds up to 1000 characters. {{shot:03|The Match panel, showing both teams and their averages, five empty stars outlined, an empty review box and a Post button.}}
  4. For Player and Team, pick who you are rating from the list at the top first. {{shot:04|The Player panel with a picker showing Ollie, that player's 0.0 average and 0 reviews, and five empty stars.}}
  5. Select Post.

Change a rating you have already left

Open Rate again and choose the same thing. Your stars and your review are already filled in, and the button now reads Update.

{{shot:05|The Team panel for KB Comets, showing four of five stars filled, the review "Best we have played all season." and an Update button, outlined.}}

Rating the referee

Referee only appears when the match had one. If nobody was assigned, you get three choices instead of four. See {{link:09.6|Assigning a referee and adding a banner or note}}.

{{shot:06|The Referee panel showing Rae KB, her 4.0 average from 3 reviews, and five empty stars outlined.}}

Remove a rating

  1. Open the team, the player or the match you rated. Select the n reviews link beside its average.
  2. Find your own review. It is the only one with a menu beside it.
  3. Select the menu, then Delete. Choose Edit to change it instead. {{shot:07|The reviews list for KB Comets with a menu open beside Pia KB's own review, offering Edit and Delete.}}

Delete does not ask twice. The rating goes the moment you select it.

What you will see

One rating each. You get one rating per thing. Rating something again replaces what you left before; it does not add a second.

The stars follow your pointer. Moving across the row lights them up as a preview. Nothing is saved until you select Post or Update.

If it does not work

  • There is no Rate button. The match has not finished. Ratings open once it has.
  • The stars will not respond. You are looking at your own player record. You cannot rate yourself.
  • The picker on the Team panel says "Player". The label is wrong; the list is teams. Choose your team from it.
  • There is no Referee row. No referee was assigned to that match.
  • You cannot find a Delete in the rating window. It is not there. Use the menu on your own review in the n reviews list.
