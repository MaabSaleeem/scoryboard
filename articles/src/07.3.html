A team's crest, banner and bio all live on one screen: Edit Team. You need to be the team's Owner or an Administrator to open it.

Change how your team looks

  1. Open Teams, select the menu at the end of the team's row, and select Edit. The first card is PROFILE APPEARANCE. The banner runs across the top and the crest sits on it. {{shot:01|The Profile appearance card showing a team banner with the crest overlaid on it, each with an edit and a delete control.}}
  2. Select the edit control on the banner. The pair of controls sit in the top right of the banner; the crest has its own pair beneath it. {{shot:02|The Profile appearance card with the banner's edit and delete controls outlined.}}
  3. Choose an image. Scoryboard opens Crop Banner so you can frame it. Select Apply & Upload to save it, or Cancel to keep the one you have. {{shot:03|The Crop Banner overlay showing the image in a crop window, with Cancel and Apply & Upload buttons.}}
  4. Scroll to TEAM INFORMATION and write a Bio. You have 150 characters, and the counter under the box tells you how many you have used. {{shot:04|The Team information card with the Bio field outlined, holding a 104-character bio and a 104/150 counter.}}
  5. Select Save Changes. The name, the size and the bio are saved together. {{shot:05|The Team information card with the Save Changes button outlined.}}

What you will see

The crest is what shows beside your team everywhere else: in your Teams list, on a match card, in a leaderboard table.

The bio shows on the team's page under TEAM BIO. A team with no bio reads "No bio added."

Changing a team's crest does not affect its bio. They are saved independently.

If it does not work

  • The image is refused. The caption says JPG, GIF or PNG, 3MB maximum. Anything larger, or any other format, is turned away.
  • The crop window will not cover the whole image. The crop is a fixed shape. Use a wide image for the banner and a square one for the crest, or accept the band the cropper takes.
  • There is no Edit on the team's row. You are not the Owner or an Administrator of that team. Ask whoever is.
  • The bio stops at 150 characters. That is the limit. Put the rest in the team's chat or a match note.
