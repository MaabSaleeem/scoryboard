Once you have chosen a team, a leaderboard or your friends list, you pick the people and fill in the request.

Before you start

Only registered players can be charged. Somebody you added by name alone has no account, so Scoryboard has nobody to send the request to.

Picking the players

  1. Tick the people you want to charge, or use Select all. {{shot:01|The Select Players window reading "Players (0/5)" with Select all outlined and Continue greyed out.}}
  2. Select all picks everybody who can be charged, which is why the count may be lower than the number of people on screen. {{shot:02|The same window reading "Players (2/5)" after Select all, with Deselect all now offered.}}
  3. Somebody with no account has an Add email button instead of a tick box. Add their email and invite them first. {{shot:03|A team member with no account, showing an Add email button where the tick box would be.}}
  4. Choose Continue.

Filling in the request

  1. Give the request a Title. You have 40 characters. A Description is optional and takes 100. {{shot:04|The Payment Request form with Title, Description, Due date and Base Price fields.}}
  2. Choose a Due date. {{shot:05|The Payment Request form with the date picker open, showing a month of days.}}
  3. Set the Base Price. This is what each person pays, before the transaction fee. It can be between £0.01 and £200.
  4. Choose Send. {{shot:06|The completed form with a title, a description, a due date of 30-09-2026 and a base price of 10, with Send outlined.}}
  5. Your request appears under REQUESTED, and everybody you picked gets it on their PAY list. {{shot:07|The REQUESTED tab listing the new request alongside the others.}}

What you will see

The Base Price is per person, not the total. Four players at £10 each raises £40.

The due date matters after the request has gone out: once it passes you can no longer add people to the request or change its wording. See {{link:17.6|Editing, cancelling and sending reminders}}.

If it does not work

  • Continue is greyed out. Nobody is selected yet. Pick at least one person.
  • Somebody is missing from the list. They are on the team by name only. Use Add email, and they can be charged once they have an account.
  • The base price is refused. It has to be at least £0.01 and no more than £200.
