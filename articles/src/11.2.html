Your statistics and your team's are two different counts. A team counts every match it played. You count only the matches you were in the lineup for. That one difference explains most of what looks wrong on these screens.

Both counts are built from matches in a leaderboard. A match outside one is not counted at all.

Find your numbers, and your team's

  1. Open Home. Your ten tiles sit under your bio. Here they read MATCHES 3. {{shot:01|The ten player statistic tiles on Home, with the MATCHES tile outlined, reading 3.}}
  2. Open your team from the TEAMS panel. Its ten tiles are on the TEAM INFO tab, and they read MATCHES 4 - the team played a fourth match that this player was not in. {{shot:02|The ten team statistic tiles on KB 11 Rovers, with the MATCHES tile outlined, reading 4.}}
  3. Select PLAYER STATS for a row per member. Everyone else on this team played four; the outlined row played three. {{shot:03|The Player Stats table on KB 11 Rovers, with Pia's row outlined: 3 matches where every other row reads 4.}}

What each tile counts

Your ten tiles:

TileWhat it counts
MATCHESLeaderboard matches you were in the lineup for
WIN, LOSS, DRAWHow those matches ended for your team
W/L RATIOWins to losses, written as a ratio. Draws are not in it
PLAYER OF THE MATCHTimes you were chosen
GOALS SCOREDGoals credited to you
ASSISTSGoals credited to somebody else where you were named the assist
CARD, red swatchRed cards you were shown
CARD, yellow swatchYellow cards you were shown

Your team's ten tiles are the same six at the start and four of its own:

TileWhat it counts
MATCHES, WIN, LOSS, DRAW, W/L RATIOEvery leaderboard match the team played, however the squad changed
CLEAN SHEETSMatches the team won without conceding. A 0-0 draw does not add one
WINNING STREAKSThe length of the team's current run of wins
GOALS / CONCEDEDGoals scored, then goals let in
CARD, red then yellowCards shown to the team's players

Under the tiles, TOP SCORER and MOST ASSISTS name one member each.

The two tiles called CARD

Both tiles are labelled CARD and the only difference is the little coloured rectangle beside the word. On the tiles the red one comes first.

In the PLAYER STATS table and in the LEADERBOARDS table it is the other way round: the yellow column comes first. Check the swatch, not the position.

What you will see

Your Matches list and your Matches tile count different things. The MATCHES panel on Home lists your teams' fixtures, including matches you did not play in. The MATCHES tile beside it counts only the ones you were in the lineup for. A team of four fixtures and a tile reading 3 is correct, not broken.

Statistics survive a lot. Your figures stay on your profile even if the team you played for is deleted.

Rank is a position, not a formula we can state. The PLAYER STATS table ranks members from 1st down. Goals weigh heavily - a player with fewer matches and more goals is placed higher - but the full ordering is not published, and two members with identical rows can be ranked differently.

Compare on the Player Stats tab is a Pro feature. See "Comparing your stats with other players".

If it does not work

  • Everything reads zero. The matches are not in a leaderboard. Nothing is counted until they are, and a finished match cannot be moved into one.
  • Your number is lower than your team's. Expected. You were not in the lineup for every match.
  • A goal is credited to the wrong player. Goals follow the match feed. Whoever ran the match can correct the event while the match is still live. Once the match has finished, nothing about it can be changed.
  • Clean sheets is 0 after a 0-0. Expected. Only wins add one.
  • The numbers have not moved since the match. See "When your statistics update after a match".
